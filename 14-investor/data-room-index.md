# Ameen Data Room Index

## Purpose

This document defines the future investor or strategic-partner data room for Ameen.

The data room should contain evidence, not marketing claims. Any document that is not validated should be clearly labeled as draft, assumption, or pending review.

## Data Room Principles

- Do not include fabricated traction.
- Do not include unsupported customer names.
- Clearly mark draft legal documents.
- Clearly mark unreviewed financial assumptions.
- Separate product evidence from roadmap promises.
- Keep sensitive code, customer data, credentials, and private documents out unless there is a controlled NDA process.

- Do not imply revenue unless signed or paid.

## Documents Not Ready Yet

The following documents should not be presented as final until validated:

- Financial model.
- Legal agreements.
- Market size analysis.
- Customer traction metrics.
- Security assurance claims.
- Framework licensing/legal opinion.
- AI accuracy benchmarks beyond current tested scope.

## Data Room Readiness Levels

### Level 0 — Internal Draft

Early planning only. Not for investors.

### Level 1 — Advisor Review

Can be shared with trusted advisors under confidentiality.

### Level 2 — Strategic Partner Review

Can be shared with serious partners after NDA.

### Level 3 — Investor Review

Can be shared with investors after core evidence exists.

## Current Recommended Level

Ameen is currently best treated as Level 0 to Level 1 until pilots and legal/security reviews mature.

## Final Rule

The Ameen data room should prove discipline.

It should show that the company understands its opportunity, its risks, its customer, its product, and its boundaries.

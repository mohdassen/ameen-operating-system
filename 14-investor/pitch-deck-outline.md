# Ameen Pitch Deck Outline

## Purpose

This outline defines the first investor or strategic-partner pitch deck for Ameen.

It should be used only after the product has a demonstrable pilot workflow, clear commercial packaging, and documented legal/security boundaries.

## Slide 1 — Title

**Ameen**

Saudi AI Compliance Readiness Platform

Supporting line:

> Upload documents. Understand readiness. Prepare with confidence.

## Slide 2 — The Problem

Compliance readiness is still manual, slow, and fragmented.

Key messages:

- Policies and evidence are scattered.
- Teams work through spreadsheets and email.
- Mapping documents to controls takes significant manual effort.
- Executives lack clear readiness visibility.
- Consultants repeat similar assessment work for every customer.

## Slide 3 — Why This Matters Now

Organizations are facing growing pressure to prove cybersecurity and regulatory readiness.

Key messages:

- More assessments and internal reviews.
- More evidence expectations.
- Arabic and English documentation complexity.
- Need for faster, more explainable readiness preparation.

## Slide 4 — The Solution

Ameen turns existing documents into explainable compliance readiness.

Flow:

1. Create organization.
2. Upload documents.
3. AI analyzes.
4. AI maps controls.
5. Customer reviews.
6. Upload evidence.
7. Readiness dashboard.

## Slide 5 — Product Demo Snapshot

Show the product workflow visually:

- Uploaded document list.
- AI control mapping.
- Gap explanation.
- Evidence recommendation.
- Readiness dashboard.
- Executive report.

## Slide 6 — What Makes Ameen Different

Ameen is differentiated by trust, focus, and simplicity.

Key points:

- Saudi-first framework strategy.
- Arabic-first experience.
- Explainable AI, not black-box scoring.
- Human approval before conclusions.
- Evidence-driven readiness dimensions.
- Customer-controlled deployment option.

## Slide 7 — First Market

Initial target segments:

- Government and semi-government.
- Higher education.
- Healthcare.
- Medium and large Saudi enterprises.
- Consultants and managed service providers.

## Slide 8 — Business Model

Initial revenue streams:

- Paid pilots.
- Annual platform subscriptions.
- Dedicated customer deployments.
- Professional services for onboarding and readiness configuration.
- Partner and consultant enablement.

## Slide 9 — Go-To-Market

Initial go-to-market motion:

1. Secure 3 to 5 design partners.
2. Convert pilots into annual contracts.
3. Build case studies.
4. Partner with consultants and managed service providers.
5. Expand framework coverage after trust is established.

## Slide 10 — Product Roadmap

Roadmap phases:

- Phase 1: Pilot readiness and ECC focus.
- Phase 2: Multi-framework readiness.
- Phase 3: Evidence lifecycle and partner workspaces.
- Phase 4: Enterprise integrations and continuous monitoring.

## Slide 11 — Trust and Risk Management

Ameen must win trust before scale.

Key points:

- Legal review required for official framework text usage.
- Human expert validation required.
- Independent security review required.
- AI output must remain explainable and human-reviewed.
- Sensitive customers can use customer-controlled deployment.

## Slide 12 — Traction

This slide should remain blank until there is real evidence.

Possible future metrics:

- Number of pilots.
- Number of documents analyzed.
- Number of controls mapped.
- Time saved versus manual mapping.
- Renewal or conversion intent.
- Customer testimonials.

Do not fabricate traction.

## Slide 13 — Competitive Landscape

Position Ameen against:

- Traditional GRC platforms.
- Consulting-led assessment projects.
- Local Saudi compliance platforms.
- AI document tools.

Key message:

> Ameen wins where customers need Saudi-first, document-driven, explainable readiness rather than a heavy GRC rollout or manual consulting-only project.

## Slide 14 — Team

This slide should present the actual founding and advisory team only.

Do not overstate roles, credentials, or employment relationships.

## Slide 15 — Ask

Possible asks depending on context:

- Design partner pilots.
- Strategic channel partnership.
- Advisory support.
- Funding conversation.
- Enterprise pilot sponsor.

## Final Pitch Message

Ameen is building the readiness layer for Saudi compliance.

The product starts with a simple action — upload documents — and turns that into explainable readiness intelligence that helps organizations prepare faster, reduce uncertainty, and build confidence before audit or assessment.

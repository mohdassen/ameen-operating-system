# Ameen Website Design Brief

## Objective

Create a professional enterprise SaaS website for Ameen that can support pilot conversations with Saudi organizations, consultants, and partners.

The website must not overpromise. It must build trust by clearly explaining what Ameen does, what it does not do, what is available today, and how pilots are controlled.

## Primary Website Goal

The website should convert qualified visitors into demo or pilot conversations.

Primary call to action:

> Request a Demo

Secondary call to action:

> Join the Design Partner Pilot

## Target Visitors

The website must speak to:

1. CISOs and cybersecurity leaders.
2. CIOs and IT leaders.
3. Compliance and risk teams.
4. Internal auditors.
5. External consultants.
6. Procurement and executive stakeholders.

## Core Message

Ameen helps Saudi organizations turn existing documents and evidence into explainable compliance readiness against Saudi frameworks.

Recommended headline:

> AI Compliance Readiness for Saudi Organizations

Recommended subheadline:

> Upload your policies, procedures, and evidence. Ameen analyzes them, maps them to official Saudi controls, highlights gaps, and produces explainable readiness reports for human review.

## Messaging Rules

Use:

- Readiness
- Assessment support
- Evidence guidance
- Explainable AI
- Human approval
- Saudi framework alignment
- Controlled pilot

Avoid:

- Certified compliant
- NCA approved
- Auditor replacement
- Guaranteed pass
- Fully automated compliance
- Legal assurance

## Homepage Structure

### 1. Hero Section

Goal: Explain Ameen in 10 seconds.

Elements:

- Headline
- Subheadline
- Demo CTA
- Pilot CTA
- Product workflow visual

### 2. Problem Section

Show why compliance is difficult today:

- Regulations are complex.
- Evidence is scattered.
- Manual assessments take weeks.
- Consultants are expensive.
- Readiness is difficult to measure continuously.

### 3. Solution Section

Show Ameen's workflow:

1. Create organization.
2. Upload documents.
3. AI analyzes.
4. Controls are mapped.
5. Customer reviews.
6. Evidence is uploaded.
7. Readiness dashboard is generated.

### 4. Product Capabilities

Show five key capabilities:

- Document intelligence
- Control mapping
- Evidence guidance
- Readiness dashboard
- Audit traceability

### 5. Saudi Frameworks

Show available and planned frameworks.

Messaging must clearly distinguish:

- Available now
- Imported/under review
- Planned roadmap

### 6. AI & Trust

Explain:

- Local analyzer option
- Explainable decisions
- Human approval
- Data handling
- Official/advisory separation

### 7. Pilot Program

Explain controlled pilot terms:

- Customer-controlled infrastructure
- Limited scope
- Local analyzer by default
- Written scope and boundaries
- Design partner feedback

### 8. CTA

Invite the visitor to request a demo or join the pilot.

## Website Pages

Minimum launch website:

1. Home
2. Product
3. AI & Trust
4. Saudi Frameworks
5. Solutions - Government
6. Solutions - Consultants
7. Pilot Program
8. Resources
9. About
10. Contact

## Design Feel

The website should feel:

- Premium
- Saudi enterprise
- Trustworthy
- Modern
- Calm
- Boardroom-ready

It should not feel:

- Experimental
- Consumer app
- AI hype
- Generic cyber vendor
- Dark hacker theme

## Visual Components

Required components:

- Hero dashboard mockup
- Simple seven-step journey diagram
- Trust pillars section
- Framework status grid
- Pilot offer card
- Executive report preview
- Control mapping preview
- Evidence guidance preview
- FAQ accordion
- Contact/demo form

## Trust Components

The website must include clear trust language:

- Ameen supports readiness assessment; it does not issue regulatory certification.
- AI recommendations require human review and approval.
- Pilot deployments should use customer-controlled infrastructure for regulated environments.
- Official framework content usage requires legal review before broad commercial publication.

## Conversion Strategy

The website should not try to close enterprise customers instantly.

The conversion should be:

Visitor → Request demo → Discovery call → Controlled pilot → Paid expansion.

## Copy Style

Write in clear executive language.

Use short sentences.

Avoid long technical explanations on the homepage.

Move details to trust, FAQ, and product pages.

## Arabic Support

The first version may be English-first, but the brand must be prepared for Arabic-first sales.

Future version should support:

- English site
- Arabic site
- Bilingual demo deck
- Arabic executive one-pager

## Final Design Principle

The website must make the visitor feel:

> This company understands Saudi compliance, respects risk, and can help us prepare without exaggeration.

## Core Government Message

Ameen is designed for organizations that need confidence before formal review.

It gives leadership a clear readiness view while giving compliance and technical teams a structured way to prepare documentation, evidence, and remediation actions.

---

## Government Pain Points

Government organizations often face these challenges:

- Policies and evidence are spread across departments.
- Teams are unsure which controls are truly covered.
- Evidence is collected manually and repeatedly.
- Readiness is difficult to explain to leadership.
- External consultants produce reports that are not always easy to maintain.
- Compliance work becomes reactive before audits.
- Arabic documents are common, but many tools are English-first.

Ameen is built to reduce this friction.

---

## How Ameen Helps

### 1. Document Intelligence

Ameen reads uploaded policies, procedures, and supporting documents, then suggests how they relate to Saudi framework requirements.

### 2. Explainable Control Mapping

Every AI suggestion should be reviewable. Users should understand why a document was mapped to a control.

### 3. Evidence Guidance

Ameen recommends what type of evidence may be required for each control, helping teams move from policy existence to practical readiness.

### 4. Human Approval

AI does not decide compliance. Government users review, approve, reject, or adjust AI suggestions.

### 5. Readiness Dashboard

Leadership can see readiness by domain, control area, evidence status, verification status, and remaining gaps.

### 6. Private Deployment Option

For sensitive environments, Ameen should be offered as a customer-controlled deployment with local analysis by default.

---

## Recommended Government Deployment Message

For regulated or sensitive entities, the recommended pilot model is:

- One customer, one dedicated deployment.
- Customer-controlled infrastructure where possible.
- Local analyzer enabled by default.
- No sensitive production data in the initial pilot unless approved by the customer.
- Written pilot scope and data handling statement.
- Human review required before any readiness output is treated as final.

---

## What Ameen Does Not Do

Ameen does not replace:

- The regulator
- Internal audit
- External audit
- Cybersecurity operations tools
- SIEM/SOC platforms
- Vulnerability scanners
- GRC ownership

Ameen supports readiness. It does not certify compliance.

## Government Use Cases

### Use Case 1: Pre-Assessment Readiness

Before a formal review, the organization uploads existing documentation and identifies likely gaps.

### Use Case 2: Evidence Preparation

Teams use Ameen to understand what evidence may be needed and where evidence is missing.

### Use Case 4: Continuous Readiness

Instead of preparing only before audits, the organization continuously updates documents, evidence, and readiness status.

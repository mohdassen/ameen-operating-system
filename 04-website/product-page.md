## Product Principle

Ameen is built around a simple principle:

> The customer should upload documents once and receive a clear, explainable readiness picture.

The product hides complexity. The customer experience stays simple.

---

## Product Capabilities

### 1. Organization Setup

Ameen starts by defining the organization and assessment scope.

**What it supports:**

- Organization profile
- Sites
- Business units
- Roles and responsibilities
- Assessment preferences
- Applicable framework baseline

**Customer value:**

The assessment starts from the customer's real context, not a generic checklist.

---

### 2. Bulk Document Upload

Customers upload the documents they already have.

**Supported examples:**

- Policies
- Procedures
- Standards
- Business continuity plans
- Risk documents
- Security reports
- Evidence files
- Screenshots
- Audit records

**Customer value:**

The customer does not need to start by filling hundreds of forms. Ameen begins with the evidence they already have.

---

### 3. AI Document Understanding

Ameen extracts and analyzes document content.

**What Ameen looks for:**

- Security policies
- Control statements
- Responsibilities
- Review cycles
- Evidence indicators
- Dates
- Approvals
- Gaps
- Missing implementation details

**Customer value:**

The platform reduces the time spent manually reading and classifying documents.

---

### 4. Control Mapping

Ameen maps documents to official controls.

**What is shown:**

- Control ID
- Control title
- Match confidence
- Rationale
- Matched document
- Supporting passage
- Review status

**Customer value:**

The customer can understand why Ameen thinks a document supports a control.

---

### 5. Human Review

AI suggestions are not final decisions.

**Workflow:**

```
AI Suggests → Human Reviews → Approve / Reject → Readiness Updates
```

**Customer value:**

Ameen accelerates compliance work while keeping accountability with the organization.

---

### 6. Evidence Management

Ameen links evidence to controls.

**Evidence can include:**

- Approved policies
- Procedures
- Configuration screenshots
- Logs
- Meeting minutes
- Review records
- Training records
- System exports

**Customer value:**

The customer builds an organized evidence library connected to official controls.

---

### 7. Four-Dimension Readiness

Ameen does not treat compliance as one simple percentage.

It separates readiness into four dimensions:

| Dimension | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Documented | Is the requirement documented? |
| Implemented | Is it actually implemented? |
| Evidenced | Is there proof? |
| Verified | Has a human reviewer verified it? |

**Customer value:**

Leadership can see the real maturity of compliance instead of a misleading single score.

---

### 8. Gap Analysis

Ameen identifies missing or weak areas.

**Examples:**

- Control has no supporting document.
- Policy exists but does not include required review frequency.
- Evidence exists but has not been verified.
- Implementation is mentioned but not proven.
- Guidance exists but official requirement is not satisfied.

**Customer value:**

The team knows what to fix first.

---

### 9. Readiness Dashboard

The dashboard summarizes progress.

**Shows:**

- Overall readiness
- Readiness by domain
- Readiness by control
- Missing evidence
- Pending reviews
- High-priority gaps
- Improvement progress

**Customer value:**

Executives get visibility without reading every control.

---

### 10. Reports

Ameen produces reports for management and assessment preparation.

**Report types:**

- Executive readiness summary
- Gap analysis report
- Evidence status report
- Control traceability report
- Improvement plan
- Assessment history

**Customer value:**

The organization can communicate readiness clearly to leadership and auditors.

---

## Why Ameen Is Trusted

Ameen is designed with safeguards:

- Official framework content is separated from advisory guidance.
- AI never modifies official regulation.
- AI suggestions require human approval.
- Every decision can be traced to a document and control.
- Guidance is labelled separately from official requirements.
- Audit history records important actions.

---

## What Ameen Is Not

Ameen is not:

- A regulator
- A certification body
- A replacement for auditors
- A SIEM
- A vulnerability scanner
- An EDR
- A ticketing system

Ameen is a readiness and decision-support platform.

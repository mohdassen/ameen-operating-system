# Ameen Website — Homepage Copy

**Document owner:** ChatGPT / Product & GTM

**Purpose:** This document defines the first version of the Ameen homepage messaging. It is written for a Saudi enterprise buyer: CIO, CISO, compliance manager, internal auditor, digital transformation leader, or executive sponsor.

## Page Objective

The homepage must make one thing clear within five seconds:

> Ameen helps Saudi organizations understand and improve compliance readiness by using AI to analyze their existing documents against official Saudi frameworks.

The homepage should not feel like a generic GRC website. It should feel like a trusted Saudi compliance product built for local regulatory reality.

```
Upload Documents → AI Analysis → Control Mapping → Readiness Dashboard
```

## Section 1 — The Problem

### Headline

**Compliance work is still too manual.**

### Copy

Saudi organizations are required to manage growing regulatory expectations across cybersecurity, data, cloud, digital transformation, and sector-specific requirements. Yet most compliance work still depends on spreadsheets, scattered documents, repeated consultant reviews, and manual evidence collection.

This creates uncertainty:

- Which controls are already covered?
- Which documents prove compliance?
- What evidence is missing?
- What should be fixed first?
- Can leadership trust the readiness score?

Ameen was built to reduce that uncertainty.

---

## Section 2 — How Ameen Works

### Headline

**From documents to readiness in one simple journey.**

### Steps

1. **Create your organization**  
   Define the basic profile of the organization and the applicable assessment scope.

2. **Upload existing documents**  
   Add policies, procedures, standards, plans, reports, and evidence files.

3. **AI analyzes the content**  
   Ameen reads the documents and identifies relevant compliance signals.

4. **Controls are mapped**  
   Documents are mapped to official controls with confidence, rationale, and traceability.

5. **Humans review AI suggestions**  
   AI assists. Humans decide. Readiness does not move without human approval.

6. **Gaps and evidence are shown**  
   Ameen explains what is covered, what is missing, and what evidence is normally expected.

7. **Leadership sees readiness**  
   Dashboards and reports show documented, implemented, evidenced, and verified readiness.

## Section 3 — Why Ameen Is Different

### Headline

**Built for trust, not just automation.**

### Differentiators

**Official-source framework content**  
Ameen's regulatory layer is built from official sources and designed for traceability.

**Explainable AI**  
Every AI suggestion must explain why it matched a document to a control.

**Human approval by design**  
AI never approves compliance by itself.

**Arabic-first experience**  
Built for Saudi organizations that operate in Arabic, English, or both.

**Evidence guidance**  
Ameen helps users understand what kind of evidence is normally used to demonstrate a control.

**Simple customer journey**  
The customer uploads documents. Ameen does the heavy work behind the scenes.

## Section 4 — Saudi Frameworks

### Headline

**Focused on Saudi compliance from day one.**

### Copy

Ameen is designed around the Saudi regulatory environment. The first supported framework is NCA ECC 2:2024, with additional Saudi frameworks planned through the same controlled content process.

### Framework Status Language

Use careful status wording:

- **Supported:** available for customer assessment.
- **Imported / under validation:** available internally but not yet active for customer assessment.
- **Planned:** on roadmap, not yet included.

Never imply regulator endorsement.

---

## Section 5 — AI With Control

### Headline

**AI assists. Humans decide.**

### Copy

Ameen uses AI to accelerate document review, control mapping, gap identification, and evidence recommendations. But compliance decisions remain under human control.

Ameen is designed so that:

- AI does not invent regulatory requirements.
- AI does not approve evidence alone.
- AI does not claim certification.
- AI suggestions remain traceable to documents and controls.

This makes AI useful without making it uncontrolled.

## Homepage Messaging Rules

- Do not claim official certification.
- Do not claim regulator endorsement.
- Do not promise 100% compliance.
- Do not say AI replaces auditors or consultants.
- Do not oversell automation that is not yet implemented.
- Always position Ameen as readiness, evidence, traceability, and decision support.

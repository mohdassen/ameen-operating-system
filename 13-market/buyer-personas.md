## Purpose

This document defines the main people Ameen must convince during sales, pilots, demos, and renewal discussions.

Ameen is not sold to one person only. Compliance platforms are bought by a committee.

---

## Persona 1: Chief Information Security Officer / Cybersecurity Leader

### What They Care About

- Compliance readiness.
- Audit pressure.
- Security risk visibility.
- Evidence quality.
- Avoiding embarrassing audit findings.
- Protecting sensitive documents.

### Pain

The CISO is often accountable for compliance results but depends on many teams to provide evidence.

### Ameen Message

> “Ameen gives you a clear, explainable readiness view from the documents and evidence your organization already has.”

### Objections

- Can AI be trusted?
- Where does the data go?
- Is the framework content accurate?
- Can this survive an auditor review?

### Proof Needed

- Local analyzer option.
- Source traceability.
- Human approval workflow.
- Audit trail.
- Security roadmap.
- Clear disclaimer that Ameen supports readiness, not certification.

---

## Persona 2: GRC / Compliance Manager

### What They Care About

- Control mapping.
- Evidence collection.
- Gap tracking.
- Reporting.
- Corrective actions.
- Reducing spreadsheet workload.

### Pain

The GRC manager spends too much time chasing documents and maintaining manual mapping tables.

### Ameen Message

> “Ameen helps you map policies and evidence to controls faster, with explanations you can review and approve.”

### Objections

- Will it create more work?
- Will the mapping be wrong?
- Can I override AI decisions?
- Can I export reports?

### Proof Needed

- Simple upload flow.
- AI suggestion review.
- Accept/reject decisions.
- Readiness dashboard.
- Evidence recommendations.

---

## Persona 3: CIO / IT Director

### What They Care About

- Executive visibility.
- Operational readiness.
- Budget justification.
- Technology risk.
- Vendor simplicity.
- Fast deployment.

### Pain

The CIO wants compliance progress without creating a huge manual project that drains IT resources.

### Ameen Message

> “Ameen turns compliance from a long manual discovery exercise into a structured readiness view executives can understand.”

### Objections

- How long does implementation take?
- Does this need many integrations?
- How much will it cost?
- Does it increase operational risk?

### Proof Needed

- Pilot scope.
- Timeline.
- Deployment model.
- No need for full integration on day one.
- Clear executive reporting.

---

## Persona 4: Internal Audit

### What They Care About

- Evidence reliability.
- Traceability.
- Accountability.
- Review history.
- Control coverage.
- Avoiding unsupported claims.

### Pain

Internal audit must validate readiness but often receives incomplete, inconsistent, or undocumented evidence.

### Ameen Message

> “Ameen does not hide the reasoning. It shows the documents, mappings, decisions, and gaps behind every readiness result.”

### Objections

- Is this an audit tool or management tool?
- Can we rely on AI output?
- Who approved each result?
- Can we see what changed?

### Proof Needed

- Audit trail.
- Human approval gate.
- Versioned documents.
- Evidence status.
- Clear distinction between AI suggestions and approved results.

---

## Persona 5: Procurement / Vendor Management

### What They Care About

- Commercial clarity.
- Contract risk.
- Data protection.
- Scope boundaries.
- Pricing.
- Support model.

### Pain

Procurement needs a clear, low-risk engagement model and cannot approve vague AI promises.

### Ameen Message

> “Ameen starts with a controlled pilot, clear scope, clear data handling, clear legal boundaries, and defined success criteria.”

### Objections

- Is this SaaS or professional service?
- What happens to our data?
- Who owns the results?
- What are the SLAs?
- Can we terminate after pilot?

### Proof Needed

- Pilot agreement.
- Data handling statement.
- SLA outline.
- Pricing structure.
- Statement of work.

---

## Persona 6: Consulting Partner / MSSP

### What They Care About

- Faster assessments.
- Repeatable delivery.
- Differentiation.
- Client reporting.
- Margin improvement.
- Multi-client operating model.

### Pain

Consultants spend too much time on repetitive document review and evidence mapping.

### Ameen Message

> “Ameen gives consultants a repeatable AI-assisted readiness engine while keeping final judgment with human experts.”

### Objections

- Will Ameen replace consultants?
- Can it support multiple clients?
- Can outputs be branded?
- Can our methodology be included?

### Proof Needed

- Partner model.
- Client isolation.
- White-label or co-branded reports later.
- Exportable findings.
- Clear boundaries between AI and consultant opinion.

## Buying Committee Summary

| Persona | Main Need | Best Proof |
|---|---|---|
| CISO | Trust and security | Local analyzer, traceability, human approval |
| GRC Manager | Faster mapping | Upload, AI suggestion, review workflow |
| CIO | Executive clarity | Dashboard, pilot timeline, low integration burden |
| Internal Audit | Evidence reliability | Audit trail and decision history |
| Procurement | Commercial control | Scope, pricing, data handling, contracts |
| Consultant Partner | Delivery speed | Repeatable assessment engine |

---

## Sales Rule

Never run a demo for only one persona.

Every Ameen demo should speak to:

- The CISO's trust concerns.
- The GRC manager's workload.
- The CIO's executive visibility.
- Internal audit's traceability requirements.
- Procurement's data and contract risk.

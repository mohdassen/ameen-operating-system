# Ameen Product Demo Script

**Document owner:** ChatGPT / Product & GTM  
**Status:** Draft v1.0  
**Purpose:** Define a 10-minute product demo script for early Saudi customers, design partners, executives, and consulting partners.

---

## Demo Goal

The demo must make the customer feel:

> I can upload my current documents and quickly understand where we stand against Saudi compliance requirements.

This is not a feature tour. It is a story.

---

## Demo Audience

Primary:

- CIO
- CISO
- Compliance manager
- Internal auditor
- Digital transformation leader
- Consultant partner

Secondary:

- Procurement
- Risk leader
- Executive sponsor

---

## Demo Time

Target: 10 minutes.

Maximum: 15 minutes.

---

## Demo Company

Use a fictional but realistic Saudi organization.

### Recommended Demo Entity

**Najd Digital Services**

Profile:

- Saudi mid-sized organization
- 900 employees
- Uses cloud and on-prem systems
- Preparing for cybersecurity readiness review
- Has policies but evidence is scattered

Do not use a real customer name.

---

## Demo Documents

Prepare 8-10 demo documents.

Recommended set:

1. Information Security Policy
2. Access Control Policy
3. Password Policy
4. Risk Management Procedure
5. Backup and Recovery Procedure
6. Incident Response Plan
7. Business Continuity Plan
8. Vendor Security Procedure
9. Security Awareness Training Record
10. Example system configuration screenshot

Include both Arabic and English documents if possible.

---

## Demo Flow

### Step 1 — Opening

Say:

> Today I will show you how Ameen turns existing documents into a clear compliance readiness picture. The goal is not to replace your team or your auditor. The goal is to help you understand what is covered, what is missing, and what evidence you need.

Do not start by explaining the database or architecture.

---

### Step 2 — Organization Setup

Show the organization page.

Say:

> We start with the organization profile and assessment scope. Ameen uses this context to prepare the readiness workspace.

Keep this short. Do not spend more than 60 seconds here.

---

### Step 3 — Upload Documents

Show document upload.

Say:

> Instead of asking the customer to answer hundreds of questions manually, Ameen starts with what the organization already has: policies, procedures, plans, reports, and evidence.

Upload the prepared documents.

Expected customer reaction:

They should immediately understand that this saves time.

---

### Step 4 — AI Classification

Show that documents are recognized.

Say:

> Ameen classifies the uploaded documents. For example, it can identify access control policies, backup procedures, incident response plans, and supporting evidence.

Important:

For Saudi customers, show Arabic classification if available.

---

### Step 5 — Control Mapping

Show mapped controls.

Say:

> Ameen then maps the documents to official controls. It does not simply give a score. It explains which document supports which control, why it matched, and what evidence it found.

Show:

- Control ID
- Control title
- Matched document
- Confidence
- Rationale

---

### Step 6 — Traceability

Open one control.

Say:

> This is the most important part. Ameen lets you trace the result from the official control to the exact document and passage that supports it.

Show:

```
Official Control → Matched Document → Supporting Passage → AI Rationale
```

This is where trust is built.

---

### Step 7 — Human Review

Approve one AI suggestion and reject another.

Say:

> AI assists, but humans decide. Ameen does not allow AI alone to move the readiness score. A reviewer must approve the suggestion.

This is critical for auditors and CISOs.

---

### Step 8 — Evidence Upload

Upload one evidence file.

Say:

> After the document supports a control, the team can upload operating evidence, such as screenshots, logs, review records, or system exports.

Show evidence linked to a control.

---

### Step 9 — Readiness Dashboard

Open dashboard.

Say:

> Readiness is shown in four dimensions: documented, implemented, evidenced, and verified. This prevents false confidence. A policy document alone does not mean the control is fully compliant.

Show:

- Overall readiness
- Domain readiness
- Missing evidence
- Pending reviews
- Gaps

---

### Step 10 — Gap Report

Show gap analysis.

Say:

> Ameen turns assessment into action. It shows what is missing and what evidence is normally expected.

Show:

- Missing controls
- Partial controls
- Recommended evidence
- Suggested remediation

---

### Step 11 — Executive Close

Say:

> In a normal assessment, this first picture can take days or weeks of manual review. Ameen gives your team a structured first-pass readiness view much faster, while keeping every decision explainable and reviewable.

---

## Demo Closing Message

Use this exact close:

> Ameen is not here to replace your compliance team or your auditor. It is here to give them a faster, clearer, and more trustworthy starting point.

---

## Questions to Ask After Demo

Ask:

1. How do you currently perform readiness assessments?
2. Where are your compliance documents stored today?
3. Which part of the process takes the most time?
4. Who reviews evidence before submission?
5. Which Saudi frameworks matter most for your organization?
6. Would a 90-day pilot help you validate this with your own documents?

---

## Demo Rules

Do not:

- Claim full compliance.
- Claim NCA endorsement.
- Hide current limitations.
- Show roadmap features as live features.
- Spend too much time on technical architecture.
- Use real customer data.

Do:

- Keep the journey simple.
- Show traceability.
- Emphasize human review.
- Use Saudi context.
- Focus on time saved and trust gained.

---

## Expected Outcome

A successful demo ends with the customer saying one of these:

- Can we test it with our documents?
- Can it support our framework scope?
- How long does a pilot take?
- Can this run in our environment?
- What is the price for a pilot?

If the customer asks these questions, the demo worked.

## 2. Executive Summary

[Customer Name] is seeking to improve compliance readiness, reduce manual assessment effort, and gain clearer visibility into evidence and gaps across applicable Saudi regulatory frameworks.

Ameen proposes an AI-assisted compliance readiness platform that enables the organization to upload existing documents, map them to selected controls, identify gaps, recommend evidence, and produce explainable readiness reports.

The proposed engagement will begin with a controlled pilot focused on agreed frameworks, selected documents, and clearly defined success criteria.

---

## 3. Customer Challenge

Common challenges Ameen helps address:

- Compliance preparation is slow and manual.
- Policies and evidence are scattered across departments.
- Teams struggle to understand which documents satisfy which controls.
- Management lacks a clear readiness dashboard.
- External consulting can be costly and periodic rather than continuous.
- Audit preparation creates pressure because evidence is not continuously maintained.

---

## 4. Proposed Solution

Ameen will provide a compliance readiness platform that supports:

- Organization setup.
- Document upload.
- AI-assisted document classification.
- Control mapping.
- Human review and approval.
- Evidence collection.
- Readiness dashboard.
- Gap analysis.
- Executive reporting.

The customer journey remains simple:

> Create Organization → Upload Documents → AI Analyzes → AI Maps Controls → Customer Reviews → Upload Evidence → Readiness Dashboard

## 6. Deployment Model

Recommended deployment for pilot:

- Dedicated environment.
- Local analyzer by default.
- External AI disabled unless approved by customer.
- Customer-controlled infrastructure preferred for sensitive data.

Deployment decision to be confirmed during kickoff.

---

## 7. Data Handling

Ameen processes customer documents and evidence only for the agreed assessment purpose.

For sensitive environments, Ameen recommends a customer-controlled deployment with local analysis by default.

Any use of external AI providers must be explicitly approved by the customer and documented in the agreement.

---

## 8. Pilot Timeline

Recommended timeline:

### Week 1: Kickoff and Setup

- Confirm scope.
- Confirm framework.
- Confirm users.
- Confirm deployment model.
- Configure environment.

### Week 2: Document Upload and Initial Analysis

- Upload selected documents.
- Run classification.
- Review control mappings.

### Week 3: Evidence and Readiness Review

- Upload evidence.
- Review gaps.
- Validate readiness dashboard.

### Week 4: Executive Review and Next Steps

- Present pilot results.
- Confirm business value.
- Agree production roadmap.

For larger pilots, extend to 60–90 days.

---

## 9. Success Criteria

The pilot will be considered successful if:

- Customer can upload and manage documents.
- Ameen identifies relevant controls and gaps.
- AI recommendations are explainable.
- Customer can review and approve mappings.
- Evidence can be attached and tracked.
- Dashboard provides useful readiness visibility.
- Final report supports management discussion.

# Ameen Founding Design Partner Program

## Purpose

The pilot program is the first commercial motion for Ameen.

Ameen is not yet positioned for broad enterprise production deployment. It is positioned for controlled paid pilots with design partners who want early access, direct influence, and a Saudi-first AI compliance platform.

## Pilot Positioning

Ameen helps Saudi organizations understand compliance readiness by analyzing their existing documents against official Saudi frameworks, identifying gaps, recommending evidence, and producing explainable readiness insights.

The pilot is designed to prove value, improve product accuracy, and build trust before full commercial launch.

## Who Should Join

Ideal pilot customers:

- Saudi government entities.
- Universities and education institutions.
- Healthcare organizations.
- Medium to large private organizations.
- Cybersecurity consulting firms.
- Managed service providers.
- Organizations preparing for NCA ECC assessment.

## Pilot Promise

During the pilot, Ameen will help the customer answer:

1. What controls appear covered by existing documents?
2. What controls are missing evidence?
3. Which documents support which controls?
4. What evidence should be prepared next?
5. What are the most important gaps to close before assessment?

## What the Pilot Includes

### 1. Onboarding Session

A guided onboarding workshop to understand:

- Organization type.
- Compliance objectives.
- Relevant frameworks.
- Existing documents.
- Preferred deployment model.
- Data sensitivity requirements.

### 2. Secure Deployment

Recommended pilot model:

- Customer-controlled infrastructure.
- Local analyzer by default.
- No external AI provider unless explicitly approved.
- Clear statement of where documents are processed.

### 3. Document Upload and Analysis

Customer uploads a selected document set such as:

- Information Security Policy.
- Password Policy.
- Access Control Policy.
- Incident Response Procedure.
- Backup Policy.
- Risk Management Policy.
- Business Continuity Plan.
- Vendor Security Procedure.

### 4. AI Mapping Review

Ameen maps documents to controls and presents:

- Suggested mappings.
- Confidence levels.
- Supporting passages.
- Rationale.
- Missing evidence.

### 5. Readiness Dashboard

Ameen produces readiness across:

- Documented.
- Implemented.
- Evidenced.
- Verified.

### 6. Pilot Findings Report

At the end of the pilot, Ameen delivers:

- Readiness summary.
- Top gaps.
- Evidence recommendations.
- Improvement roadmap.
- Product feedback summary.

## What the Pilot Does Not Claim

The pilot does not claim:

- Regulatory certification.
- Formal compliance approval.
- Auditor acceptance.
- Legal assurance.
- NCA endorsement.

Ameen supports readiness and evidence preparation.

## Pilot Success Criteria

A pilot is successful if:

1. The customer understands their readiness faster than before.
2. AI mappings are useful and explainable.
3. Evidence recommendations are practical.
4. The compliance team saves meaningful time.
5. The customer can identify clear next steps.
6. The customer is willing to continue beyond the pilot.

## Pilot Duration

Recommended duration:

- 30 days for a focused proof of value.
- 60 days for a department-level pilot.
- 90 days for a broader organizational pilot.

## Pilot Pricing Strategy

Do not discount to zero unless there is a strategic reason.

Free pilots attract weak commitment.

Recommended model:

- Paid pilot fee.
- Discounted conversion to annual subscription.
- Founding design partner benefits.
- Optional professional services package.

## Design Partner Benefits

Founding design partners receive:

- Early access.
- Roadmap influence.
- Priority support.
- Preferential commercial terms.
- Option to co-create case study after approval.
- Access to new Saudi frameworks before general availability.

## Internal Pilot Qualification Questions

Before accepting a pilot, ask:

1. Does the organization have a real compliance need?
2. Do they have documents ready to upload?
3. Is there an executive sponsor?
4. Is there a compliance owner?
5. Are they willing to provide feedback?
6. Are they open to becoming a reference later?
7. Are their security requirements compatible with current deployment options?

## Pilot Exit Options

At the end of the pilot:

1. Convert to annual subscription.
2. Extend pilot with larger scope.
3. Convert to managed compliance service.
4. Stop and archive customer data according to agreement.

## Commercial Rule

A pilot should never be treated as a free demo.

A pilot is a structured commercial engagement designed to prove value, reduce risk, and create the first trusted customer references for Ameen.

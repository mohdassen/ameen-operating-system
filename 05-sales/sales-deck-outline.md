# Ameen Sales Deck Outline

**Document owner:** ChatGPT / Product & GTM  
**Status:** Draft v1.0  
**Purpose:** Define the first enterprise sales deck structure for Ameen. This will later become a PowerPoint / Google Slides deck.

---

## Deck Goal

The sales deck must make a Saudi executive or CISO believe:

> Ameen can help my organization understand compliance readiness faster, with more traceability, and less manual effort.

The deck should not oversell certification or imply regulator endorsement.

---

## Recommended Length

15-18 slides.

Enough to feel enterprise-grade, not too long for a first meeting.

---

## Slide 1 — Cover

**Title:** Ameen  
**Subtitle:** AI Compliance Readiness for Saudi Organizations  
**Tagline:** Trust Every Assessment  
**Visual:** premium Saudi enterprise / compliance visual, not hacker imagery

---

## Slide 2 — The Problem

Saudi organizations face increasing cybersecurity, data, cloud, and digital transformation requirements.

But readiness work is still mostly manual.

Pain points:

- Scattered policies and evidence
- Manual control mapping
- Expensive consultant dependency
- Slow readiness assessments
- Unclear ownership
- Weak executive visibility

---

## Slide 3 — Why Current Approaches Fail

Current process:

```
Spreadsheets + shared folders + consultant review + manual evidence chasing
```

Problems:

- Hard to know what is complete
- Hard to prove why a control is marked ready
- Hard to repeat assessments consistently
- Hard to maintain readiness continuously

---

## Slide 4 — Introducing Ameen

Ameen is an AI-powered Saudi compliance readiness platform.

It helps organizations:

- Upload existing documents
- Analyze them using AI
- Map documents to official controls
- Identify gaps
- Recommend evidence
- Track review and readiness

---

## Slide 5 — The Simple Journey

Show the core workflow:

```
Create Organization → Upload Documents → AI Analysis → Review → Evidence → Readiness Dashboard
```

Message:

The customer experience stays simple. The intelligence happens behind the scenes.

---

## Slide 6 — Built for Saudi Frameworks

Start with NCA ECC.

Roadmap includes additional Saudi frameworks such as CCC, DCC, CSCC, OTCC, TCC, OSMACC, DGA Qiyas, PDPL, and SAMA where applicable.

Important wording:

Ameen assesses readiness against published frameworks. It does not claim regulator certification or endorsement.

---

## Slide 7 — Official Content and Traceability

Explain the trust chain:

```
Official Framework → Control → Document → Passage → AI Rationale → Human Review → Readiness
```

Message:

Customers can understand why Ameen reached a conclusion.

---

## Slide 8 — AI That Assists, Humans Decide

Ameen AI helps with:

- Classification
- Mapping
- Gap detection
- Evidence recommendations
- Remediation suggestions

But:

- AI never approves compliance by itself
- Human review is required
- Every recommendation must be explainable

---

## Slide 9 — Readiness Dimensions

Show the four dimensions:

| Dimension | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Documented | Requirement appears in approved documentation |
| Implemented | Implementation is stated or assessed |
| Evidenced | Supporting proof exists |
| Verified | Reviewer confirmed the evidence |

Message:

A policy alone is not full compliance.

## Sales Deck Rules

- Do not claim certification.
- Do not claim regulator approval.
- Do not attack competitors directly.
- Do not show features that do not exist as if they exist.
- Clearly separate current capability from roadmap.
- Keep the message simple: upload documents, get readiness clarity.

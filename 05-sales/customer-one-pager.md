# Ameen

## AI-Powered Saudi Compliance Readiness

Ameen helps organizations understand, evidence, and improve compliance readiness against Saudi regulatory frameworks using explainable AI and a simple document-first workflow.

---

## The Problem

Compliance readiness is often slow, manual, and expensive.

Organizations already have policies, procedures, and evidence, but they struggle to answer simple questions:

- Which controls are covered?
- Which controls are missing?
- What evidence is required?
- What should management prioritize?
- Are we ready for an audit?

Traditional assessments depend heavily on spreadsheets, workshops, consultants, and scattered evidence.

---

## The Ameen Approach

Ameen makes the journey simple:

> Upload documents. Review AI mapping. Understand readiness. Close gaps.

Ameen analyzes existing documents, maps them to selected compliance controls, identifies gaps, recommends evidence, and generates readiness dashboards and reports.

---

## What Ameen Does

- Reads uploaded policies, procedures, and evidence.
- Classifies documents by purpose.
- Maps documents to relevant controls.
- Explains why a document supports a control.
- Shows missing documentation and evidence.
- Recommends next actions.
- Supports human review and approval.
- Produces executive readiness reports.

---

## Why It Matters

Ameen helps organizations:

- Reduce manual assessment effort.
- Prepare faster for audits.
- Improve visibility for leadership.
- Reuse existing documents more effectively.
- Track evidence continuously.
- Reduce dependency on one-time spreadsheet assessments.
- Make compliance decisions more explainable.

## Built for Saudi Compliance

Ameen is designed for Saudi organizations and Saudi regulatory expectations.

The platform starts with Saudi cybersecurity and compliance frameworks, with a roadmap to expand across additional governance, privacy, and sector-specific requirements.

---

## Trust and Privacy

Ameen is designed around trust:

- Human approval before final decisions.
- Clear distinction between official content, customer evidence, and AI guidance.
- Local analysis option for sensitive environments.
- Dedicated deployment model for regulated customers.
- No claim of regulator certification or audit replacement.

## What Ameen Is Not

Ameen is not:

- A regulator.
- A certification authority.
- A legal advisor.
- A formal auditor.
- A SIEM, EDR, scanner, or ticketing system.

Ameen is a readiness platform that helps organizations understand and improve their compliance position.

# Ameen Roles and Responsibilities

## Document Status

Owner: CEO / Operations  
Applies to: Internal operating model  
Version: 1.0  
Status: Draft operating standard

---

## Purpose

This document defines the key roles needed to build, sell, support, and govern Ameen.

In the early stage, one person may hold multiple roles. The purpose of this document is not to hire immediately. The purpose is to prevent responsibility gaps as Ameen moves from product development to commercial pilots.

---

## Core Operating Rule

Every major area must have one clear owner.

Ameen must avoid the common startup problem where everyone discusses strategy, but nobody owns execution.

---

## Strategic Roles

### Founder / CEO

Purpose:

Own the company direction, market focus, customer trust, and final business decisions.

Responsibilities:

- Define company vision.
- Approve commercial positioning.
- Own key customer and partner relationships.
- Approve pricing and pilot terms.
- Make go/no-go decisions.
- Approve risk acceptance.
- Represent Ameen externally.

Decisions owned:

- Market focus.
- Product category.
- Commercial model.
- Strategic partnerships.
- Funding or investment decisions.
- Enterprise launch readiness.

---

### Product Owner

Purpose:

Own what Ameen should be, what it should not be, and how the customer experiences it.

Responsibilities:

- Own product roadmap.
- Protect simple customer journey.
- Prioritize features.
- Define product requirements.
- Own product messaging.
- Own demo storyline.
- Maintain Ameen Operating System product sections.
- Translate customer feedback into product decisions.

Decisions owned:

- Feature priority.
- Customer journey changes.
- UI/UX direction.
- Product packaging.
- Demo scope.

Important rule:

The product owner must protect the frozen customer journey unless a formal decision changes it.

---

### Engineering Lead

Purpose:

Own implementation, security, quality, and technical truth.

Responsibilities:

- Build the platform.
- Maintain code quality.
- Own QA and regression testing.
- Own technical architecture.
- Fix security defects.
- Maintain framework import pipeline.
- Maintain AI providers and local analyzer.
- Report engineering risks honestly.

Decisions owned:

- Technical architecture within product scope.
- Engineering implementation plan.
- Code-level quality gates.
- Technical risk severity.

Important rule:

Engineering must not invent product strategy silently. Product and commercial decisions must be documented.

---

### Compliance Content Lead

Purpose:

Own the accuracy, review, and safe use of compliance content.

Responsibilities:

- Review official framework interpretation.
- Review Arabic and English clause alignment.
- Review evidence expectations.
- Review knowledge layer guidance.
- Validate compliance wording.
- Prevent unsafe regulatory claims.

Decisions owned:

- Compliance content acceptance.
- Framework wording risk.
- Guidance confidence level.
- Evidence expectation quality.

Important rule:

Ameen must separate official regulatory text from Ameen advisory guidance.

---

### Security and Trust Lead

Purpose:

Own customer data protection, security posture, and trust controls.

Responsibilities:

- Review application security.
- Own risk register for security issues.
- Track penetration testing.
- Review tenant isolation.
- Review audit trail completeness.
- Own incident response readiness.
- Review AI provider data exposure risks.

Decisions owned:

- Security risk classification.
- Production readiness from security perspective.
- Incident response escalation.
- Data exposure risk acceptance.

---

## Commercial Roles

### Sales Lead

Purpose:

Own pipeline, customer conversations, pilot conversion, and revenue generation.

Responsibilities:

- Identify target accounts.
- Qualify opportunities.
- Run discovery calls.
- Present product value.
- Manage proposal process.
- Track objections.
- Close pilot agreements.
- Coordinate executive demos.

Decisions owned:

- Prospect qualification.
- Sales stage movement.
- Commercial follow-up timing.

Important rule:

Sales must not make compliance, legal, security, or roadmap promises that are not approved.

---

### Customer Success Lead

Purpose:

Own customer onboarding, training, adoption, and pilot health.

Responsibilities:

- Run onboarding.
- Train customer teams.
- Track pilot progress.
- Capture feedback.
- Manage support expectations.
- Coordinate weekly pilot reviews.
- Identify expansion opportunities.

Decisions owned:

- Customer onboarding plan.
- Training schedule.
- Pilot health classification.
- Customer adoption risks.

---

### Marketing Lead

Purpose:

Own market education, brand awareness, website content, and campaign execution.

Responsibilities:

- Maintain website content.
- Create launch campaigns.
- Create thought leadership.
- Create LinkedIn content.
- Create white papers.
- Package customer stories.
- Maintain brand consistency.

Decisions owned:

- Marketing calendar.
- Content themes.
- Campaign execution.

---

## Legal and Operations Roles

### Legal Advisor

Purpose:

Review and approve legal documents, claims, data handling terms, and regulatory risk.

Responsibilities:

- Review pilot agreement.
- Review SaaS agreement.
- Review data processing terms.
- Review privacy policy.
- Review regulatory content use.
- Review commercial claims.

Decisions owned:

- Legal approval.
- Contract risk.
- Regulatory wording risk.

Important rule:

Internal legal drafts are not approved legal documents until reviewed by qualified counsel.

---

### Operations Lead

Purpose:

Keep company execution structured.

Responsibilities:

- Maintain operating rhythm.
- Maintain decision log.
- Maintain risk register.
- Track deliverables.
- Track customer commitments.
- Coordinate cross-functional work.

Decisions owned:

- Operating cadence.
- Delivery tracking.
- Internal documentation hygiene.

## Simple RACI Model

RACI definitions:

- Responsible: Does the work.
- Accountable: Owns the final decision.
- Consulted: Provides input before decision.
- Informed: Needs to know outcome.

### Product Feature Decision

- Responsible: Product Owner.
- Accountable: CEO.
- Consulted: Engineering Lead, Compliance Content Lead, Customer Success.
- Informed: Sales and Marketing.

### Security Fix Decision

- Responsible: Engineering Lead.
- Accountable: Security and Trust Lead.
- Consulted: Product Owner, CEO.
- Informed: Customer Success if customer-facing.

### Pricing Decision

- Responsible: Sales Lead.
- Accountable: CEO.
- Consulted: Product Owner, Customer Success, Legal Advisor.
- Informed: Marketing.

### Pilot Launch Decision

- Responsible: Customer Success Lead.
- Accountable: CEO.
- Consulted: Product Owner, Engineering Lead, Legal Advisor, Security and Trust Lead.
- Informed: Sales and Marketing.

### Website Messaging Decision

- Responsible: Marketing Lead.
- Accountable: Product Owner.
- Consulted: CEO, Sales Lead, Legal Advisor.
- Informed: Engineering.

### Framework Publication Decision

- Responsible: Compliance Content Lead.
- Accountable: CEO.
- Consulted: Engineering Lead, Product Owner, Legal Advisor.
- Informed: Sales and Customer Success.

---

## Early-Stage Role Consolidation

During the earliest stage, roles may be combined:

- CEO may also act as Sales Lead and Product Sponsor.
- Product Lead may own brand, website, and AOS.
- Engineering Lead may own technical operations and QA.
- External advisors may cover legal and compliance review.

But even when one person holds multiple roles, the responsibilities must remain separate.

---

## Responsibility Gaps to Avoid

Ameen must avoid unclear ownership for:

- Legal approval of NCA content use.
- Arabic clause review.
- Customer data handling.
- Pilot support commitments.
- AI provider configuration.
- Security risk acceptance.
- Roadmap promises to customers.
- Sales claims about certification.

---

## Final Standard

Ameen must scale responsibilities before it scales headcount.

Clear ownership is one of the foundations of customer trust.

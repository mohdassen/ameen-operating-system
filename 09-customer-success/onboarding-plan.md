# Ameen Customer Onboarding Plan

## Document Status

Owner: Customer Success  
Applies to: Pilot customers and early enterprise customers  
Version: 1.0  
Status: Draft operating standard

---

## Purpose

This document defines how Ameen should onboard a new customer in a controlled, professional, and trust-first way.

The purpose of onboarding is not only to activate the software. The purpose is to help the customer reach a first credible readiness view without confusion, false confidence, or unnecessary manual work.

---

## Onboarding Principle

Ameen onboarding must follow one simple rule:

> The customer should never feel they are configuring a compliance engine. They should feel they are being guided through a readiness assessment.

Ameen must hide internal complexity and keep the customer journey simple:

1. Create organization.
2. Upload documents.
3. AI analyzes documents.
4. AI maps documents to controls.
5. Customer reviews suggestions.
6. Customer uploads evidence.
7. Readiness dashboard is produced.

This journey must not be changed without a formal product decision.

---

## Target Customer for Initial Onboarding

The first onboarding model is designed for:

- Saudi government entities.
- Universities and public sector organizations.
- Medium and large Saudi enterprises.
- Advisory firms running controlled pilots.
- Organizations preparing for NCA ECC readiness.

Ameen should not yet be positioned as a self-service mass-market SaaS product for regulated production data.

---

## Recommended Pilot Onboarding Model

For the first commercial pilots, Ameen should use a guided onboarding model.

The recommended model is:

- One customer.
- One controlled deployment.
- Customer-approved document scope.
- Local analyzer by default unless otherwise approved.
- No unnecessary sensitive data uploaded.
- Human review required before any readiness output is treated as final.

## Onboarding Phases

### Phase 1: Commercial Kickoff

Objective: Align expectations before system access.

Activities:

- Confirm customer sponsor.
- Confirm operational owner.
- Confirm pilot scope.
- Confirm target framework.
- Confirm document categories allowed in pilot.
- Confirm what Ameen will and will not assure.
- Confirm whether data stays in customer-controlled infrastructure.

Exit criteria:

- Pilot scope approved.
- Data handling statement accepted.
- Pilot success criteria agreed.

---

### Phase 2: Technical Readiness

Objective: Prepare the environment safely.

Activities:

- Confirm deployment model.
- Confirm admin users.
- Confirm organization creation.
- Confirm AI analyzer mode.
- Confirm backup and access responsibilities.
- Confirm no production-sensitive documents are uploaded outside approved scope.

Exit criteria:

- Customer environment ready.
- Admin users created.
- Framework scope enabled.
- Analyzer mode confirmed.

---

### Phase 3: Document Intake

Objective: Upload the first assessment document set.

Recommended starting documents:

- Information Security Policy.
- Access Control Policy.
- Password Policy.
- Risk Management Policy.
- Business Continuity Plan.
- Incident Response Policy.
- Vendor Security Policy.
- Asset Management Policy.
- Backup Policy.
- Change Management Policy.

Rules:

- Do not ask the customer to upload everything on day one.
- Start with 10 to 20 documents.
- Avoid highly sensitive operational files during the first pilot unless approved.
- Keep document names understandable.

Exit criteria:

- First approved document set uploaded.
- Documents classified by Ameen.
- Unclear documents flagged for customer review.

---

### Phase 4: AI Mapping Review

Objective: Convert AI suggestions into customer-approved assessment inputs.

Activities:

- Review mapped controls.
- Review why each document was mapped.
- Approve strong matches.
- Reject weak or irrelevant matches.
- Mark areas requiring more evidence.

Rules:

- AI must not be treated as final authority.
- Customer approval must remain visible.
- Readiness must be evidence-driven.

Exit criteria:

- First control mapping set reviewed.
- Rejected mappings documented.
- Missing areas identified.

---

### Phase 5: Evidence Upload

Objective: Move from policy presence to evidence-backed readiness.

Evidence examples:

- Screenshots.
- Configuration exports.
- Meeting minutes.
- Approval records.
- Reports.
- Logs.
- Process documents.
- Audit samples.

Rules:

- Evidence should be tied to specific controls.
- Evidence should be reviewed by humans.
- Ameen can guide evidence expectations, but must not certify compliance.

Exit criteria:

- Initial evidence uploaded.
- Evidence linked to controls.
- Evidence gaps visible.

---

### Phase 6: Readiness Review

Objective: Produce a credible first readiness view.

Ameen should show readiness across the approved dimensions:

- Documented.
- Implemented.
- Evidenced.
- Verified.

Rules:

- Policy alone must not equal full readiness.
- Verified readiness requires human validation.
- Executive dashboard must clearly show limitations.

Exit criteria:

- First readiness dashboard reviewed.
- Key gaps discussed.
- Next actions agreed.

---

### Phase 7: Executive Playback

Objective: Show business value to customer leadership.

Recommended structure:

1. What was assessed.
2. What documents were used.
3. What Ameen found.
4. Where the organization appears strong.
5. Where gaps remain.
6. What evidence is missing.
7. What decisions are required.
8. Recommended next 30 days.

Exit criteria:

- Executive sponsor understands value.
- Pilot continuation or expansion decision is made.

---

## Customer Onboarding Success Criteria

The onboarding is successful if the customer can say:

- We understand our readiness better than before.
- We can see which controls are supported by documents.
- We can see what evidence is missing.
- We understand that AI suggestions require review.
- We trust the traceability of the assessment.
- We know what to do next.

---

## What Onboarding Must Avoid

Ameen onboarding must avoid:

- Asking the customer to configure complex control structures.
- Claiming regulatory certification.
- Claiming NCA approval or endorsement.
- Uploading uncontrolled sensitive data in early pilots.
- Treating AI output as final audit opinion.
- Selling broad GRC workflows before the readiness journey is mature.

---

## Internal Owner Checklist

Before onboarding starts:

- Pilot agreement approved.
- Data handling statement approved.
- Customer sponsor identified.
- Customer admin identified.
- Scope confirmed.
- Deployment model confirmed.
- Analyzer model confirmed.
- Framework scope confirmed.
- Onboarding meeting scheduled.

After onboarding ends:

- Readiness dashboard reviewed.
- Customer feedback captured.
- Product issues logged.
- Commercial next step agreed.
- Case study potential assessed.

---

## Final Standard

Ameen onboarding is not a training session.

It is the first proof that Ameen can turn customer documents into a trusted compliance readiness picture.

# Ameen Training Academy

## Document Status

Owner: Customer Success / Product Marketing  
Applies to: Customers, partners, internal team  
Version: 1.0

---

## Purpose

Ameen Training Academy defines the learning materials needed to help customers, partners, and internal teams understand and use Ameen correctly.

The academy must not become generic cybersecurity training. It must focus on the Ameen way of turning documents and evidence into explainable readiness.

---

## Academy Objective

The academy has one objective:

> Help every user understand what Ameen found, why it found it, and what action should be taken next.

---

## Training Principles

### 1. Simple Before Complete

Most users do not need to understand every internal detail of compliance frameworks. Training should begin with the simple journey:

1. Upload documents.
2. Review AI suggestions.
3. Add evidence.
4. Read the readiness dashboard.
5. Take action.

### 2. Explain Readiness Dimensions Early

Users must understand that policy documents alone are not full compliance.

Ameen readiness is viewed through dimensions:

- Documented.
- Implemented.
- Evidenced.
- Verified.

### 3. AI Is an Assistant, Not an Auditor

Training must repeat this message:

> AI helps analyze and suggest. Humans review and decide.

### 4. Use Customer Language

Training should avoid unnecessary jargon. Where technical or regulatory terms are necessary, explain them with examples.

### 5. Show, Then Let Users Practice

Every training module should include:

- Explanation.
- Live example.
- Customer exercise.
- Common mistakes.

## Core Training Modules

### Module 1: Introduction to Ameen

Outcome:

Users understand what Ameen is and what problem it solves.

Topics:

- Why compliance readiness is difficult.
- Ameen's document-first approach.
- Saudi-first framework strategy.
- Human review principle.
- What Ameen does not claim.

---

### Module 2: The Ameen Customer Journey

Outcome:

Users understand the full workflow from organization setup to dashboard.

Topics:

- Create organization.
- Upload documents.
- AI classification.
- AI control mapping.
- Customer review.
- Evidence upload.
- Readiness dashboard.

---

### Module 3: Understanding Readiness Dimensions

Outcome:

Users understand why readiness is not a single percentage.

Topics:

- Documented readiness.
- Implemented readiness.
- Evidenced readiness.
- Verified readiness.
- Why policy alone is not enough.
- How to interpret gaps.

---

### Module 4: Reviewing AI Suggestions

Outcome:

Users can approve, reject, and question AI mapping suggestions.

Topics:

- What a strong match looks like.
- What a weak match looks like.
- How to read explanation text.
- When to reject a suggestion.
- When to upload more evidence.

---

### Module 5: Evidence Management

Outcome:

Users know what types of evidence strengthen readiness.

Topics:

- Policy versus evidence.
- Screenshots.
- Reports.
- Logs.
- Approvals.
- Process records.
- Technical exports.
- Evidence quality mistakes.

---

### Module 6: Executive Dashboard

Outcome:

Users can explain the dashboard to leadership.

Topics:

- Overall readiness.
- Control-level readiness.
- Missing evidence.
- Unverified areas.
- Risk-based next actions.
- Reporting boundaries.

---

### Module 7: Safe Use of AI in Compliance

Outcome:

Users understand how to use AI responsibly.

Topics:

- AI strengths.
- AI limitations.
- Local analyzer versus external providers.
- Human approval.
- Data handling.
- Avoiding false confidence.

---

## Certification Path

Ameen may later create role-based certificates, but they should be internal product certifications, not regulatory certifications.

Possible future certificates:

- Ameen Certified User.
- Ameen Certified Reviewer.
- Ameen Certified Partner Consultant.
- Ameen Certified Customer Admin.

Important wording:

Ameen certification means the person understands the Ameen product. It does not mean the person is certified by NCA or any regulator.

---

## Common Training Mistakes to Avoid

Avoid:

- Teaching the framework before teaching the workflow.
- Explaining too many features at once.
- Letting customers believe AI replaces review.
- Using legal or certification language.
- Treating dashboard percentage as final compliance status.
- Training only administrators and ignoring executives.

---

## Final Standard

The academy should make Ameen feel safe, clear, and useful.

A trained Ameen user should be able to say:

> I understand where our readiness stands, why Ameen reached that view, and what we need to do next.

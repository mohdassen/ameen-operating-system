# Ameen Vision

## Vision
To become the trusted AI-powered compliance platform for Saudi Arabia.

## Mission
Help organizations achieve continuous compliance through explainable AI.

## North Star
Every Saudi organization should be able to upload its documents, understand its compliance posture, and prepare for audits with confidence.

## Core Principles
1. Trust before features.
2. Official frameworks are the source of truth.
3. AI assists, humans decide.
4. Keep the customer journey simple.
5. Build for Saudi Arabia first, then expand.

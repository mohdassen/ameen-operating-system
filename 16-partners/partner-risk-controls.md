# Partner Risk Controls

**Document owner:** Business / Governance  
**Status:** Draft v1  
**Purpose:** Define the risks created by channel partners and the controls Ameen must apply before partners sell, deliver, or support customers.

---

## 1. Why Partner Risk Matters

Ameen is a trust product.

One partner making a false claim can damage the brand, create legal exposure, and reduce customer confidence.

The partner risk model exists to protect:
- Customer trust.
- Product credibility.
- Legal positioning.
- Data protection.
- Market reputation.
- Long-term enterprise value.

---

## 2. Main Partner Risks

### Risk 1: Overclaiming Compliance

Partner says Ameen certifies compliance or guarantees audit success.

Control:
- Mandatory approved wording.
- Legal disclaimer in all proposals.
- Review first three proposals.

### Risk 2: Regulator Misrepresentation

Partner implies NCA, DGA, SAMA, or another authority approved Ameen without formal basis.

Control:
- Zero-tolerance claim policy.
- Approved framework wording only.
- Immediate suspension for violation.

### Risk 3: Data Mishandling

Partner uploads real customer documents into unapproved environments or external AI providers.

Control:
- Data handling statement.
- Customer approval before upload.
- Local analyzer default for sensitive pilots.
- No real customer data in demo environments.

### Risk 4: Unsupported Framework Selling

Partner sells framework support that is not production-ready.

Control:
- Published framework status list.
- Proposal framework gate.
- Sales collateral version control.

### Risk 5: Poor Delivery Quality

Partner presents AI outputs without professional review.

Control:
- Human review requirement.
- Delivery checklist.
- Report quality gate.
- Partner scorecard.

### Risk 6: Brand Dilution

Partner changes messaging, visuals, pricing, or report language.

Control:
- Approved collateral pack.
- Brand usage guide.
- Proposal review.
- Version-controlled templates.

---

## 3. Partner Approval Stages

### Stage 1: Candidate

Partner is under evaluation.

Allowed:
- Introductory discussions.
- NDA.
- High-level demo.

Not allowed:
- Customer selling.
- Public announcement.
- Independent demo.

### Stage 2: Registered Partner

Partner can refer opportunities.

Allowed:
- Referral.
- Approved introduction language.

Not allowed:
- Independent delivery.
- Custom proposals without review.

### Stage 3: Trained Partner

Partner has completed enablement.

Allowed:
- Approved demo.
- Co-sell.
- Proposal with Ameen review.

### Stage 4: Certified Delivery Partner

Partner can deliver Ameen-supported readiness projects under agreed rules.

Allowed:
- Delivery.
- Managed readiness service.
- Customer workshops.

Required:
- Quality scorecard.
- Periodic review.
- Escalation discipline.

---

## 4. Mandatory Partner Controls

Every partner must have:
- Signed agreement.
- Approved messaging guide.
- Data handling obligations.
- Framework support matrix.
- Pilot scope rules.
- Demo data rules.
- Escalation path.
- Termination clause.

---

## 5. Proposal Review Rules

Ameen must review:
- First proposal from each partner.
- Any proposal to government or regulated customers.
- Any proposal with customer-controlled deployment.
- Any proposal referencing official regulatory frameworks.
- Any proposal including external AI provider use.

---

## 6. Partner Scorecard

Score partners monthly on:
- Messaging accuracy.
- Proposal quality.
- Customer feedback.
- Delivery quality.
- Data handling discipline.
- Support quality.
- Revenue contribution.
- Strategic alignment.

---

## 7. Escalation Levels

### Level 1: Coaching

Minor mistake, no customer harm.

Action:
- Correct language.
- Retrain partner.

### Level 2: Restricted Selling

Repeated mistakes or proposal quality issues.

Action:
- Require Ameen review for all deals.
- Suspend independent demo rights.

### Level 3: Suspension

Serious misrepresentation, data risk, or trust violation.

Action:
- Suspend partner activity.
- Notify affected customer if required.
- Executive review.

### Level 4: Termination

Major breach.

Action:
- Terminate partner agreement.
- Remove partner from materials.
- Preserve evidence.

---

## 8. Non-Negotiable Rules

Partners must never:
- Claim certification.
- Claim regulator approval without formal evidence.
- Upload customer data without permission.
- Use real customer data in demos.
- Sell unsupported frameworks.
- Remove disclaimers.
- Present AI output as final audit judgment.

---

## 9. Final Rule

Revenue never comes before trust.

Ameen should reject partner revenue if it creates legal, data, or credibility risk.

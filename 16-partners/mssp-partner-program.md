# MSSP Partner Program

**Document owner:** Business / Partnerships  
**Status:** Draft v1  
**Purpose:** Define how managed security service providers can use Ameen to create recurring compliance readiness services.

---

## 1. Program Purpose

Managed security service providers already support customers continuously.

Ameen can help them add a recurring compliance readiness layer on top of security operations.

The core idea:

> MSSPs monitor security. Ameen helps them monitor compliance readiness.

---

## 2. Why MSSPs Matter

MSSPs can help Ameen reach customers that need recurring support but do not have strong internal compliance teams.

Typical customer pain:
- Evidence is scattered.
- Policies are outdated.
- Controls are reviewed only before audits.
- Security operations and compliance are disconnected.
- Management wants monthly visibility.

Ameen gives MSSPs a way to turn compliance readiness into a managed monthly service.

---

## 3. MSSP Service Offering

Recommended packaged service:

# Ameen Managed Readiness Service

Monthly service including:
- Document readiness review.
- Evidence status tracking.
- Control gap monitoring.
- Executive readiness dashboard.
- Monthly compliance readiness report.
- Follow-up action register.

---

## 4. Service Tiers

### Basic Readiness Monitoring

For small and medium customers.

Includes:
- One framework.
- Monthly dashboard review.
- Evidence status report.
- Basic gap list.

### Advanced Readiness Advisory

For larger customers.

Includes:
- Multiple departments.
- Evidence improvement guidance.
- Monthly workshop.
- Remediation tracking.
- Executive summary.

### Dedicated Compliance Readiness Office

For regulated or strategic customers.

Includes:
- Dedicated deployment.
- Customer-controlled infrastructure option.
- Local analyzer default.
- Formal governance rhythm.
- Executive reporting.
- Steering committee support.

---

## 5. MSSP Responsibilities

The MSSP is responsible for:
- Customer relationship.
- Service delivery.
- Monthly review meetings.
- Evidence follow-up.
- Human interpretation of findings.
- Maintaining service discipline.
- Escalating product issues to Ameen.

Ameen is responsible for:
- Platform capability.
- Product updates.
- Approved reporting templates.
- Partner enablement.
- Technical escalation.
- Trust and data handling guidelines.

---

## 6. Deployment Models

### Shared MSSP Environment

Use only for low-sensitivity customers after security model is mature.

### Dedicated Customer Environment

Preferred for regulated customers.

### Customer-Controlled Environment

Recommended for government, banks, healthcare, and sensitive organizations.

### Local Analyzer Mode

Recommended default for pilots involving sensitive documents.

---

## 7. Reporting Model

Monthly report should include:
- Overall readiness trend.
- Controls improved this month.
- Controls still weak.
- Missing evidence.
- Documents requiring update.
- Human-reviewed recommendations.
- Next month priorities.

Reports must clearly state:

> This is a readiness report, not a regulatory certification or audit opinion.

---

## 8. Commercial Model

Ameen can charge MSSPs by:
- Customer organization.
- Framework package.
- Deployment model.
- Document volume.
- Support level.

MSSP may add its own managed service fee for advisory and follow-up.

Ameen should protect minimum pricing to avoid market dilution.

---

## 9. MSSP Enablement

MSSPs need:
- Service packaging guide.
- Monthly report template.
- Customer onboarding checklist.
- Evidence review checklist.
- Data handling statement.
- Sales deck.
- Demo script.
- Objection handling guide.
- Escalation process.

---

## 10. Risks

Main risks:
- MSSP sells Ameen as a certification tool.
- Customer assumes Ameen replaces audit.
- MSSP uploads sensitive documents into incorrect environment.
- Partner over-customizes reports.
- Weak service delivery damages Ameen brand.

Controls:
- Mandatory training.
- Approved templates.
- Data handling rules.
- Partner scorecard.
- First three customers reviewed by Ameen.

---

## 11. Ideal First MSSP Pilot

The first MSSP pilot should involve:
- One trusted MSSP.
- One selected customer.
- One framework.
- Limited document scope.
- Monthly report simulation.
- Clear success criteria.

---

## 12. Strategic Decision

Ameen should not rush into broad MSSP distribution.

The right sequence is:

1. Direct pilots.
2. Consulting co-sell.
3. MSSP managed readiness pilots.
4. Formal MSSP program.

MSSP scale is valuable only after the product, reports, and trust boundaries are proven.

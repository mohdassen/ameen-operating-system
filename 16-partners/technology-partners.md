# Technology Partner Strategy

**Document owner:** Business / Partnerships  
**Status:** Draft v1  
**Purpose:** Define which technology partnerships can strengthen Ameen's trust, deployment, security, and enterprise adoption.

---

## 1. Strategic Purpose

Technology partners should help Ameen solve trust and deployment problems, not distract the product roadmap.

The purpose of technology partnerships is:

> Make Ameen easier to deploy, safer to operate, and more credible for regulated customers.

---

## 2. Priority Technology Partner Categories

### Saudi Cloud and Hosting Providers

Purpose:
- Customer-controlled hosting.
- Local deployment options.
- Data residency confidence.
- Government and enterprise trust.

### System Integrators

Purpose:
- Enterprise deployment.
- Infrastructure readiness.
- Identity integration.
- Customer environment setup.

### Private Cloud Providers

Purpose:
- Dedicated customer infrastructure.
- On-premises or sovereign hosting.
- Local analyzer operation.

### Document Management Platforms

Purpose:
- Future integration with existing policy and evidence repositories.
- Reduce manual upload.
- Improve ongoing evidence refresh.

### Identity Providers

Purpose:
- Future SSO and MFA integration.
- Enterprise access control.
- Audit readiness.

---

## 3. Partnerships Not Needed Immediately

Ameen should avoid early partnerships with:
- Generic AI platforms that weaken privacy messaging.
- Marketplaces before product maturity.
- Broad app ecosystems before core readiness is proven.
- Vendors that require major feature refactoring.
- Partners asking for deep integrations before commercial proof.

---

## 4. Partner Selection Criteria

A technology partner should improve at least one of these:
- Customer trust.
- Security posture.
- Deployment speed.
- Data residency comfort.
- Government adoption.
- Enterprise procurement readiness.
- Supportability.

If a partnership only creates marketing noise, postpone it.

---

## 5. Deployment Partner Model

For early regulated customers, Ameen should support:
- One deployment per customer.
- Customer-controlled infrastructure.
- Local analyzer as default.
- External AI disabled unless explicitly approved.
- Written data handling statement.

Technology partners can help package this as a repeatable deployment service.

---

## 6. Integration Roadmap

### Phase 1: No Deep Integrations

Focus:
- Product stability.
- Pilot deployments.
- Manual upload workflow.
- Customer-controlled infrastructure.

### Phase 2: Enterprise Basics

Add:
- SSO/MFA.
- Read audit events.
- Evidence download controls.
- Deployment automation.
- Basic API boundaries.

### Phase 3: Evidence Source Integrations

Add integrations with:
- Document repositories.
- Ticketing tools.
- Asset inventories.
- Cloud evidence sources.
- Security control evidence sources.

### Phase 4: Ecosystem

Build a controlled partner ecosystem after product trust is established.

---

## 7. Co-Marketing Rules

Co-marketing must not imply:
- Regulator endorsement.
- Certification authority.
- Audit replacement.
- Guaranteed compliance.

Approved co-marketing position:

> Ameen and partner help organizations deploy and operate AI-assisted compliance readiness workflows in a controlled environment.

---

## 8. Technology Partner Deliverables

Each technology partner should have:
- Deployment reference architecture.
- Security responsibility matrix.
- Data flow diagram.
- Support escalation path.
- Customer onboarding checklist.
- Joint demo scenario.
- Commercial model.

---

## 9. First Technology Partner Target

The first technology partner should help with deployment trust, not product features.

Best first target:
- A Saudi hosting/private cloud/system integration partner that can support customer-controlled deployments.

Reason:
- This directly addresses Ameen's current commercial risk around regulated production data and shared deployment trust.

---

## 10. Strategic Rule

Do not integrate before there is a repeatable customer problem.

Ameen should integrate only when:
- Multiple customers request it.
- It reduces manual work significantly.
- It improves trust or adoption.
- It does not weaken the simple customer journey.

---

## 11. Final Position

Technology partnerships should make Ameen safer and easier to buy.

The first technology partnership priority is not AI.
It is trusted deployment.

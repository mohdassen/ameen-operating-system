# Reseller Enablement

**Document owner:** Business / Partnerships  
**Status:** Draft v1  
**Purpose:** Define the materials, training, controls, and operating process needed before any reseller can represent Ameen to customers.

---

## 1. Enablement Principle

A reseller should not sell Ameen until they can explain it accurately.

The core rule:

> No reseller is allowed to create their own Ameen story.

They must use approved positioning, approved disclaimers, and approved demo flow.

---

## 2. Minimum Reseller Pack

Every reseller must receive:

1. Ameen one-pager.
2. Product positioning guide.
3. Approved elevator pitch.
4. Sales deck.
5. Demo script.
6. Pilot proposal template.
7. Pricing guardrails.
8. Objection handling guide.
9. Data handling statement.
10. Legal disclaimer language.
11. Partner FAQ.
12. Escalation process.

---

## 3. Training Modules

### Module 1: What Ameen Is

Reseller must understand:
- Ameen is a Saudi AI Compliance Readiness Platform.
- Ameen analyzes uploaded documents.
- Ameen maps documents to controls.
- Ameen highlights gaps and evidence needs.
- Ameen supports readiness, not certification.

### Module 2: What Ameen Is Not

Reseller must understand:
- Not SIEM.
- Not GRC replacement for all workflows.
- Not regulator certification.
- Not audit opinion.
- Not legal advice.
- Not automatic compliance.

### Module 3: Demo Delivery

Reseller must follow the approved demo story:
- Create organization.
- Upload documents.
- AI analyzes.
- AI maps controls.
- Human reviews.
- Evidence uploaded.
- Readiness dashboard presented.

### Module 4: Trust Boundaries

Reseller must explain:
- Customer-controlled deployment option.
- Local analyzer option.
- Human approval.
- Legal disclaimer.
- Data handling scope.

---

## 4. Reseller Approval Gate

Before selling, reseller must:
- Complete product training.
- Complete trust and disclaimer training.
- Deliver a mock demo.
- Submit a sample customer email.
- Submit a sample proposal.
- Receive Ameen approval.

---

## 5. Sales Rules

Reseller may:
- Introduce Ameen to qualified customers.
- Use approved sales deck.
- Run approved demo.
- Propose pilots using approved scope.
- Escalate technical questions to Ameen.

Reseller may not:
- Change legal claims.
- Promise certification.
- Promise regulator approval.
- Create unofficial pricing.
- Use customer data in demo without approval.
- Represent unsupported frameworks as production-ready.

---

## 6. Proposal Requirements

Every reseller proposal must state:
- Deployment model.
- Framework scope.
- Document scope.
- AI provider mode.
- Data handling approach.
- Human review responsibility.
- What is assured.
- What is not assured.

---

## 7. Demo Environment Rules

Resellers may only use:
- Ameen-approved demo data.
- Synthetic documents.
- Approved screenshots.
- Approved demo tenant.

They must not upload real customer documents into demo environments.

---

## 8. Lead Registration

To avoid conflict:
- Partner registers opportunity.
- Ameen confirms acceptance.
- Partner receives protected involvement for agreed period.
- Expired or inactive deals return to open status.

---

## 9. Reseller Scorecard

Track:
- Qualified leads.
- Demo quality.
- Proposal quality.
- Compliance with approved messaging.
- Customer feedback.
- Closed pilots.
- Renewal potential.
- Support issues created.

---

## 10. Termination Triggers

Ameen may suspend reseller activity if partner:
- Misrepresents product capabilities.
- Makes unsupported compliance claims.
- Violates data handling rules.
- Ignores approved pricing guardrails.
- Damages customer trust.

---

## 11. First Enablement Goal

The first reseller should be treated as a controlled experiment.

Goal:
- One trained partner.
- One approved demo.
- One co-reviewed proposal.
- One pilot opportunity.
- Lessons captured before expanding.

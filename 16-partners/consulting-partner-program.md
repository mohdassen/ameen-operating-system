# Consulting Partner Program

**Document owner:** Business / Partnerships  
**Status:** Draft v1  
**Purpose:** Define how cybersecurity and GRC consultants can use Ameen to accelerate Saudi compliance readiness work without weakening trust or legal positioning.

---

## 1. Program Purpose

Consultants are not enemies of Ameen.

They are one of the fastest routes to market when the partnership is structured correctly.

Ameen helps consultants:
- Reduce manual document review time.
- Standardize readiness assessment quality.
- Produce more explainable reports.
- Track evidence more consistently.
- Serve more customers without lowering quality.

The program purpose is:

> Turn trusted consultants into Ameen-powered compliance readiness partners.

---

## 2. Ideal Consulting Partner

The ideal partner has:
- Saudi cybersecurity or GRC delivery experience.
- Familiarity with NCA, SAMA, DGA, PDPL, or related Saudi frameworks.
- Existing relationships with regulated or semi-regulated customers.
- A delivery team that can review AI outputs professionally.
- Willingness to follow Ameen's approved trust language.

---

## 3. Partner Roles

### Ameen Provides

- Platform access.
- Demo environment.
- Pilot template.
- Sales collateral.
- Approved messaging.
- Training.
- Support escalation.
- Product roadmap visibility.

### Partner Provides

- Customer relationship.
- Advisory work.
- Control interpretation.
- Human review.
- Evidence review.
- Customer workshops.
- Final consulting recommendations.

---

## 4. What Partners May Say

Approved language:

> We use Ameen to accelerate document analysis, control mapping, evidence tracking, and readiness reporting against Saudi compliance frameworks.

Approved language:

> Ameen supports explainable readiness assessment. Final compliance interpretation remains subject to human review and the customer's audit context.

---

## 5. What Partners Must Not Say

Partners must not claim:
- Ameen certifies compliance.
- Ameen is approved by NCA unless formally confirmed.
- AI output is equivalent to an auditor opinion.
- Uploading documents automatically makes the customer compliant.
- Ameen replaces consultants, auditors, legal counsel, or regulator judgment.

---

## 6. Delivery Model

### Step 1: Discovery

Partner identifies customer pain, frameworks, deadlines, stakeholders, and current evidence maturity.

### Step 2: Controlled Pilot

Ameen is deployed under a written scope. Sensitive documents should remain under customer-approved handling rules.

### Step 3: Document Upload

Customer uploads selected documents for pilot analysis.

### Step 4: AI Mapping

Ameen maps documents to controls and identifies gaps.

### Step 5: Human Review

Partner reviews results before presenting conclusions.

### Step 6: Readiness Workshop

Partner presents findings with Ameen dashboard and report.

### Step 7: Remediation Plan

Partner converts readiness gaps into practical improvement actions.

---

## 7. Commercial Options

### Referral Model

Partner introduces opportunity. Ameen owns sale.

### Co-Sell Model

Ameen and partner present together. Revenue is split according to agreement.

### Partner Delivery Model

Partner sells consulting package. Ameen is licensed as part of the delivery.

### Managed Readiness Model

Partner runs Ameen monthly for the customer and provides advisory follow-up.

---

- Demo script.
- Pilot proposal template.
- Objection handling guide.

- Data handling statement.
- Delivery checklist.

- Partner FAQ.

---

## 9. Certification Levels

### Registered Partner

Basic partner with access to collateral and referral model.

### Delivery Partner

Trained partner allowed to deliver Ameen-supported assessments.

### Strategic Partner

Joint go-to-market partner with co-sell plan and executive alignment.

---

## 10. Partner Quality Controls

Ameen must review:
- First customer proposal.
- First pilot scope.
- First executive report.
- First customer presentation.
- Any claim involving official frameworks or regulator relationship.

---

## 11. Partner Risk

Main risks:
- Overpromising capabilities.
- Treating readiness as certification.
- Uploading sensitive customer documents without approval.
- Editing reports without traceability.
- Selling beyond product maturity.

Mitigation:
- Approved messaging.
- Legal boundaries.
- Partner training.
- Delivery review gates.
- Partner scorecard.

---

## 12. First Partner Target

The first consulting partner should not be the largest possible firm.

It should be the most trusted, practical, and disciplined partner willing to run a controlled pilot with clear scope.

Ameen should learn with one excellent partner before scaling the program.

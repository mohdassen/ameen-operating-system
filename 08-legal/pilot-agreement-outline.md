# Pilot Agreement Outline

**Ameen Operating System**  
**Document Owner:** Commercial / Legal  
**Status:** Draft for legal review  
**Version:** 0.1

---

## Purpose

This document defines the recommended structure for Ameen's pilot agreement with design partners and early customers.

The pilot agreement should protect Ameen, clarify customer expectations, limit scope, define data handling, and avoid overpromising compliance outcomes.

---

## Recommended Pilot Positioning

The pilot should be positioned as a controlled evaluation of Ameen's ability to support compliance readiness, document intelligence, evidence mapping, and executive reporting.

The pilot should not be positioned as a formal audit, regulator-approved certification, or legal compliance opinion.

---

## Parties

The agreement should identify:

- Ameen legal entity or sponsoring commercial entity.
- Customer legal entity.
- Authorized business owner.
- Authorized technical owner.
- Authorized compliance or cybersecurity owner.

---

## Pilot Objective

Suggested wording:

> The objective of the pilot is to evaluate Ameen's ability to analyze selected customer documents, map them to agreed compliance controls, identify readiness gaps, recommend evidence, and generate readiness reports within the agreed pilot scope.

---

## Pilot Scope

The agreement should define:

- Frameworks included.
- Number of organizations or business units.
- Number of users.
- Number of uploaded documents.
- Number of evidence items.
- Whether external AI providers are enabled.
- Whether local analyzer mode is required.
- Whether customer-controlled infrastructure is required.
- Whether production, non-production, or synthetic data is allowed.

Recommended first pilot scope:

- One customer organization.
- One deployment.
- One primary framework.
- Limited controlled document set.
- Local analyzer by default.
- External AI disabled unless customer explicitly approves.

---

## Pilot Duration

Recommended duration:

- 60 days for light pilot.
- 90 days for design partner pilot.

The agreement should include start date, end date, extension process, and conversion discussion timing.

---

## Deployment Model

Pilot deployments should prefer:

1. Customer-controlled infrastructure.
2. Dedicated deployment per customer.
3. Local analyzer by default.
4. No shared regulated production data unless security controls are contractually accepted.

If cloud-hosted by Ameen, the agreement must specify hosting location, data handling, access controls, support access, and deletion obligations.

---

## Data Handling

The agreement should define:

- What data may be uploaded.
- Whether sensitive documents are permitted.
- Whether personal data is permitted.
- Data retention period.
- Data deletion process.
- Backup handling.
- Support access rules.
- Logging and audit requirements.
- AI provider processing rules.

Recommended default:

> Customer should not upload highly sensitive, classified, or regulated production data during pilot unless a written data protection and security addendum has been approved.

---

## AI Usage

The agreement should define:

- AI providers allowed.
- Whether external AI calls are allowed.
- Whether customer data may leave the deployment environment.
- Whether customer API keys are used.
- Whether Ameen-managed API keys are used.
- Whether AI outputs can be stored.
- Human review requirements.

Recommended default:

> Pilot starts with deterministic/local analysis unless the customer explicitly approves external AI processing in writing.

---

## Deliverables

Typical pilot deliverables:

- Pilot kickoff.
- User onboarding.
- Document upload and classification.
- Control mapping review.
- Readiness dashboard.
- Gap summary.
- Executive readiness report.
- Pilot findings workshop.
- Commercial conversion proposal.

---

## Customer Responsibilities

Customer should provide:

- Pilot sponsor.
- Compliance owner.
- Technical contact.
- Selected documents.
- Framework selection.
- Review and feedback sessions.
- Timely approval of findings.
- Access to agreed environment if customer-hosted.

---

## Ameen Responsibilities

Ameen should provide:

- Pilot setup.
- Product access.
- Onboarding support.
- Basic configuration.
- Product walkthrough.
- Issue handling during pilot.
- Final pilot summary.

---

## Success Criteria

Example success criteria:

- Customer can upload agreed documents.
- Ameen classifies documents correctly enough for customer evaluation.
- Ameen maps documents to relevant controls with explainable reasoning.
- Customer can review AI proposals.
- Customer can upload or attach evidence.
- Customer receives a readiness view useful for internal decision-making.
- Customer understands what is assured and what is not assured.

---

## Exclusions

The agreement should clearly exclude:

- Formal audit opinion.
- Legal advice.
- Regulator certification.
- Guaranteed compliance status.
- Penetration testing unless separately contracted.
- Full managed compliance services unless included in a separate SOW.

---

## Fees

The pilot may be:

- Paid pilot.
- Discounted design partner pilot.
- Strategic unpaid pilot with strict limits.

Recommended position:

Ameen should prefer paid pilots because paid pilots create commitment, seriousness, and commercial validation.

---

## Intellectual Property

Ameen should retain ownership of:

- Product.
- Platform design.
- AI logic.
- Templates.
- Framework mapping methodology.
- Improvements derived from aggregated lessons.

Customer should retain ownership of:

- Uploaded documents.
- Evidence files.
- Customer-specific reports.
- Customer confidential information.

---

## Feedback Rights

The agreement should allow Ameen to use customer feedback to improve the product, while protecting customer confidential information.

Public references, logos, case studies, or testimonials should require written approval.

---

## Confidentiality

The pilot agreement should include confidentiality obligations or reference a separate NDA.

---

## Limitation of Liability

The agreement should include reasonable limitation of liability language approved by counsel.

---

## Termination

Either party should be able to terminate the pilot under defined conditions, including security concerns, breach, non-cooperation, or convenience with notice.

---

## End-of-Pilot Process

At the end of the pilot:

1. Ameen provides final pilot report.
2. Customer provides feedback.
3. Parties discuss conversion to paid subscription or enterprise deployment.
4. Data is retained, exported, or deleted according to agreement.

---

## Required Legal Review

This outline is not a final legal contract.

It must be reviewed and converted into a formal agreement by qualified legal counsel before customer use.

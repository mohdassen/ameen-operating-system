# Legal Disclaimer

**Ameen Operating System**  
**Document Owner:** Commercial / Legal  
**Status:** Draft for legal review  
**Version:** 0.1

---

## Purpose

This document defines the standard legal disclaimer language that should appear in Ameen sales materials, proposals, pilot agreements, reports, product screens, and customer-facing documentation.

Ameen must be positioned as a compliance readiness and evidence intelligence platform, not as a regulator, law firm, certification authority, or audit replacement.

---

## Core Disclaimer

Ameen provides AI-assisted compliance readiness analysis, evidence mapping, and control guidance based on uploaded customer documents and configured regulatory frameworks.

Ameen does not provide legal advice, regulatory approval, certification, or formal audit assurance. Any output generated by Ameen must be reviewed and approved by the customer's authorized compliance, risk, legal, security, or audit representatives before being relied upon for regulatory, contractual, or audit purposes.

---

## Saudi Regulatory Framework Disclaimer

Ameen may assess customer readiness against Saudi regulatory frameworks and cybersecurity controls as published by relevant authorities.

Reference to any Saudi regulatory framework, including but not limited to NCA frameworks, DGA requirements, SAMA requirements, SDAIA-related requirements, or PDPL-related requirements, does not imply endorsement, certification, approval, partnership, or authorization by the issuing authority unless expressly stated in a written agreement issued by that authority.

Ameen should use language such as:

> Ameen assesses readiness against the selected framework as configured in the platform.

Ameen should avoid language such as:

> Certified by the regulator.
>
> Approved by the regulator.
>
> Guarantees compliance.
>
> Replaces the auditor.
>
> Produces official certification.

---

## AI Output Disclaimer

Ameen may use deterministic analysis, local AI, or external AI providers depending on deployment configuration.

AI-generated outputs may include document classifications, control mappings, evidence recommendations, readiness observations, remediation suggestions, and draft reports.

AI outputs are advisory and must be reviewed by humans. Ameen should clearly separate:

1. Official framework content.
2. Customer-provided evidence.
3. Ameen-generated guidance.
4. AI-generated observations.
5. Human-approved findings.

---

## Customer Responsibility

The customer remains responsible for:

- Accuracy and completeness of uploaded documents.
- Determining which frameworks apply to the organization.
- Reviewing AI outputs before use.
- Approving final control status.
- Confirming implementation of controls.
- Maintaining evidence.
- Engaging qualified legal, audit, cybersecurity, and regulatory advisors where required.

---

## Report Disclaimer

All readiness reports generated by Ameen should include the following statement:

> This report is an AI-assisted compliance readiness assessment based on documents and evidence made available to Ameen at the time of analysis. It is not a legal opinion, formal audit report, regulatory certification, or guarantee of compliance. Final interpretation, approval, and regulatory use remain the responsibility of the customer and its authorized advisors.

---

## Pilot Disclaimer

During pilots, Ameen should be positioned as an evaluation environment unless the pilot agreement explicitly states otherwise.

Recommended pilot wording:

> The pilot is designed to evaluate Ameen's ability to support compliance readiness, document analysis, evidence mapping, and reporting within an agreed scope. Pilot results are not intended to serve as formal regulatory submissions or audit opinions unless separately reviewed and approved by qualified professionals.

## Required Legal Review

This document is a commercial and product draft only.

Before use with customers, it must be reviewed by qualified legal counsel familiar with Saudi commercial law, technology contracting, data protection, regulatory compliance, and intellectual property obligations.

## Operating Rule

Ameen must sell confidence, speed, clarity, and readiness.

Ameen must not sell certainty, certification, regulator approval, or legal assurance unless those capabilities are formally obtained and contractually supported.

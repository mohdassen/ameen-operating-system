# Ameen Product Roadmap

**Document owner:** Product Leadership  
**Status:** Working Draft  
**Audience:** Founder, Engineering, Sales, Customer Success, Design Partners

---

## Purpose

This roadmap defines how Ameen becomes commercially useful without losing the simple customer journey:

> Create Organization → Upload Documents → AI Analyzes → AI Maps Controls → Customer Reviews → Upload Evidence → Readiness Dashboard

The roadmap is not a list of random features. It is a trust-building sequence.

---

## Roadmap Principle

Ameen should not add complexity faster than it adds trust.

Every roadmap item must answer at least one of these questions:

1. Does it make the assessment more trusted?
2. Does it make the customer journey simpler?
3. Does it reduce audit preparation effort?
4. Does it make Ameen safer for regulated customers?
5. Does it help convert pilots into paid customers?

If the answer is no, the item should wait.

---

## Phase 0: Foundation Already Achieved

Ameen has already established the base platform direction:

- Organization creation
- Document upload
- AI-assisted document analysis
- Control mapping
- Evidence upload
- Readiness dashboard
- Saudi framework foundation
- Human approval model
- Audit trail foundation
- Arabic-first direction
- Local analyzer option

This phase proves the product concept.

---

## Phase 1: Controlled Paid Pilot Readiness

**Goal:** Make Ameen safe and credible enough for controlled pilots.

### Priority Outcomes

- One customer, one deployment model ready.
- Local analyzer is default for sensitive customers.
- Legal disclaimers are visible and consistent.
- Customer data handling is documented.
- Assessment outputs clearly separate AI assistance from formal assurance.
- Pilot onboarding and success process is repeatable.

### Product Capabilities

- Pilot workspace setup
- Demo-ready onboarding flow
- Document upload workflow refinement
- Assessment results explanation
- Evidence guidance
- Exportable readiness report
- Clear AI confidence and human review status
- Framework source/provenance display

### Not Included Yet

- Broad public SaaS launch
- Multi-tenant regulated production
- Claims of certification
- Automated compliance approval
- Large framework expansion without review

---

## Phase 2: Trust Hardening

**Goal:** Make Ameen acceptable to CISOs, auditors, procurement, and data protection teams.

### Product Capabilities

- Encryption at rest for database and files
- Read-event audit logging
- Per-customer AI configuration model
- Stronger access control and admin governance
- Evidence lifecycle tracking
- Exportable audit evidence pack
- Load/performance gate
- Independent security review preparation

### Business Impact

This phase moves Ameen from "interesting pilot" to "credible enterprise product."

---

## Phase 3: Saudi Framework Expansion

**Goal:** Expand beyond ECC while preserving trust.

### Candidate Frameworks

- ECC
- CCC
- TCC
- OSMACC
- CSCC
- OTCC
- DCC
- DGA/Qiyas readiness
- PDPL readiness
- Sector-specific frameworks where commercially justified

### Required Gate Before Publishing Any Framework

No framework should become customer-facing until it passes:

1. Official source recorded.
2. Source digest recorded.
3. Arabic/English content reviewed.
4. Clause counts verified.
5. Importer validation passed.
6. Product UI labels are accurate.
7. Legal usage position is documented.
8. Human subject-matter review is complete or explicitly marked as pending.

---

## Phase 4: Enterprise Workflow

**Goal:** Support real compliance teams without becoming a heavy GRC tool.

### Capabilities

- Assign owners to controls
- Assign evidence tasks
- Review and approval workflow
- Commenting and evidence notes
- Due dates and reminders
- Executive report exports
- Control-level action plans
- Dashboard filters by domain, owner, readiness, and risk

### Product Boundary

Ameen should remain an AI compliance readiness platform.

It should integrate with enterprise tools later, but it should not become a generic ticketing system.

---

## Phase 5: Consultant and Partner Mode

**Goal:** Allow consultants and service providers to use Ameen across customers.

### Capabilities

- Consultant workspace
- Customer portfolio view
- Reusable assessment templates
- Branded reports
- Partner-managed pilots
- Role separation between consultant and customer

### Business Impact

This can turn consulting firms from competitors into distribution partners.

---

## Phase 6: Continuous Compliance Intelligence

**Goal:** Move from one-time readiness assessments to continuous compliance monitoring.

### Capabilities

- Change tracking across documents
- Re-assessment when policies change
- Evidence expiry tracking
- Framework update tracking
- Readiness trend over time
- Executive compliance health score

### Long-Term Position

Ameen becomes the place organizations check before every audit, board update, or regulatory readiness review.

---

## Roadmap Decision Rule

When in doubt, choose the roadmap item that increases trust first.

Ameen wins if customers believe the assessment, not if the product has the most screens.

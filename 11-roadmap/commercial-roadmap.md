# Ameen Commercial Roadmap

**Document owner:** Founder / Go-To-Market  
**Status:** Working Draft  
**Audience:** Founder, Sales, Product, Customer Success, Partners

---

## Commercial Strategy

Ameen should not launch as a public SaaS immediately.

The correct commercial path is:

> Controlled pilots → Design partners → Paid annual deployments → Partner channel → Broader platform expansion

This protects trust, creates customer evidence, and avoids overpromising before legal, security, and review gates are complete.

---

## Phase 1: Design Partner Pilots

**Target:** 3 to 5 Saudi organizations

### Ideal Pilot Customers

- Government entities
- Universities
- Healthcare organizations
- Medium and large Saudi enterprises
- Consulting firms serving regulated customers

### Pilot Positioning

Ameen is offered as a controlled readiness assessment platform, not as a certified audit tool.

### Pilot Objectives

- Prove the document-to-readiness journey.
- Validate Arabic document understanding.
- Validate evidence expectations.
- Build customer case studies.
- Identify procurement and security objections early.
- Convert at least 2 pilots into annual customers.

### Pilot Commercial Model

Pilot pricing should reflect value but remain easy to approve.

Suggested range:

- Light pilot: SAR 25,000 to 50,000
- Standard pilot: SAR 50,000 to 90,000
- Supported pilot: SAR 90,000 to 150,000

Pricing depends on scope, deployment model, support effort, number of frameworks, number of documents, and reporting expectations.

---

## Phase 2: Annual Customer Conversion

**Target:** Convert successful pilots into paid annual deployments.

### Conversion Trigger

A pilot should convert when the customer can clearly say:

- Ameen found useful gaps.
- The dashboard made readiness visible.
- The evidence guidance saved time.
- The assessment was explainable.
- The customer wants to repeat the process.

### Annual Packaging

- Professional
- Enterprise
- Government Dedicated
- Consultant Partner Edition

### Expansion Motions

- Add more frameworks.
- Add more departments.
- Add more entities or subsidiaries.
- Add consultant reporting.
- Add customer-specific readiness reports.

---

## Phase 3: Reference Customers

**Target:** Build credibility through real customer proof.

### Required Assets

- Anonymous pilot results
- Before/after readiness improvements
- Customer testimonial where possible
- One-page case study
- Demo based on realistic Saudi organization scenario
- Security and data handling statement
- Legal disclaimer pack

### Market Signal

Before aggressive sales, Ameen needs proof that the platform works in real customer environments.

---

## Phase 4: Partner Channel

**Target:** Turn consultants and managed service providers into distribution partners.

### Partner Types

- Cybersecurity consultants
- GRC consultants
- Managed service providers
- Cloud and infrastructure providers
- Audit preparation advisors

### Partner Value

Ameen helps partners deliver assessments faster, more consistently, and with better evidence traceability.

### Commercial Models

- Referral fee
- Reseller margin
- Partner-managed deployments
- Co-branded assessment packages
- White-labeled reporting where commercially approved

---

## Phase 5: Enterprise and Government Scale

**Target:** Larger annual contracts with customer-controlled infrastructure.

### Required Before This Phase

- Legal review complete
- Security hardening complete
- Independent security review planned or completed
- Arabic content review complete
- GRC practitioner review complete
- Strong support model
- Clear data handling commitments

### Sales Motion

Enterprise and government customers will buy trust, not features.

The sales motion must emphasize:

- Customer-controlled deployment
- Local analysis option
- Explainability
- Human approval
- Official framework provenance
- Audit evidence readiness

---

## Commercial Risks

### Risk 1: Selling Before Trust Is Ready

If Ameen is sold as a final compliance assurance tool too early, trust can be damaged.

### Risk 2: Competitors With Existing Relationships

Large consultants and Saudi GRC vendors may have customer relationships. Ameen must win by being faster, simpler, more explainable, and partner-friendly.

### Risk 3: Legal Ambiguity

Regulatory content usage and claims must be reviewed before broad marketing.

### Risk 4: Security Objections

Government and regulated customers will ask where documents go, who can access them, and how AI is used. Ameen must have simple, direct answers.

---

## Commercial North Star

Ameen should become the default AI readiness layer for Saudi compliance.

The first goal is not thousands of users.

The first goal is:

> Five serious Saudi design partners who trust the product enough to use it on real readiness work.

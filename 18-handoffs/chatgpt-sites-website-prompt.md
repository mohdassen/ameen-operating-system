# ChatGPT Sites Website Prompt

Use this prompt in ChatGPT Work with @Sites to create the first Ameen website prototype.

---

@Sites Build a professional website prototype for Ameen.

## Product
Ameen is a Saudi AI Compliance Readiness Platform. It helps Saudi organizations upload policies, procedures, and evidence, analyze them against official Saudi compliance controls, review AI mapping suggestions, upload evidence, and track readiness through a clear dashboard.

## Website Goal
Create a professional landing website for demo and pilot conversion. The website should feel trusted, Saudi-focused, enterprise-ready, and modern. It should not feel like a generic AI SaaS template.

## Primary Audience
- Saudi government entities
- Universities and higher education institutions
- Healthcare organizations
- Medium and large Saudi enterprises
- Consulting firms and MSSPs that support compliance programs

## Core Positioning
Ameen is not a generic GRC platform. Ameen is a document-first Saudi compliance readiness platform that turns existing documents into explainable control readiness.

## One-Liner
Upload documents once. Get traceable compliance readiness against official Saudi controls.

## Hero Section
Headline: AI Compliance Readiness for Saudi Arabia
Subheadline: Upload your policies and evidence. Ameen analyzes them against Saudi compliance controls, identifies gaps, recommends evidence, and gives your team a clear readiness dashboard.
Primary CTA: Request Demo
Secondary CTA: Join Pilot Program
Trust line: Built for explainable AI, human review, and evidence-driven readiness.

## Required Pages / Sections
Create a multi-section website with these sections:
1. Home / hero
2. The compliance problem
3. How Ameen works
4. Product capabilities
5. AI and trust
6. Saudi frameworks
7. Pilot program
8. Target customers
9. FAQ
10. Request demo form

## How Ameen Works
Keep this journey simple and visible:
1. Create Organization
2. Upload Documents
3. AI Analyzes
4. AI Maps Controls
5. Customer Reviews
6. Upload Evidence
7. Readiness Dashboard

## Four Readiness Dimensions
Show that Ameen separates readiness into:
- Documented
- Implemented
- Evidenced
- Verified

A policy alone should not equal compliance.

## Trust Message
Ameen is built around trust:
- Official-source framework provenance where legally permitted
- Arabic-first document understanding
- Explainable AI recommendations
- Human approval before compliance conclusions
- Evidence-driven readiness
- Customer-controlled deployment options
- Local analyzer option for sensitive environments

## NCA / ECC Safe Wording
Use only careful language:
- Ameen helps assess readiness against NCA ECC 2:2024 controls as published by NCA.
- Ameen supports compliance readiness preparation.
- Ameen helps identify gaps and evidence needs.

Do not claim:
- NCA approved
- NCA certified
- Regulator endorsed
- Guaranteed compliance
- Replaces auditors
- Provides legal advice
- Automatically certifies an organization

- Provide feedback that shapes the product

CTA: Request Pilot Discussion

## FAQ Questions
Include answers for:
1. Does Ameen certify compliance?
Answer: No. Ameen supports readiness assessment and evidence preparation. It does not certify compliance or replace auditors.

2. Can Ameen work with Arabic documents?
Answer: Ameen is designed for Arabic-first and English document workflows.

3. Can sensitive customers avoid external AI?
Answer: Ameen is designed to support customer-controlled deployment and local analyzer options for sensitive environments.

4. What is the best way to start?
Answer: Start with a controlled pilot focused on one organization and one framework scope.

5. Who should use Ameen?
Answer: Compliance, cybersecurity, risk, governance, internal audit, and IT leadership teams preparing for regulatory readiness.

## Tone
Use confident but careful language. Avoid hype. Avoid legal or regulatory overclaims.

Create a polished website prototype with responsive layout, modern design, clear copy, and a request demo form. Make it suitable for a Saudi executive buyer and a CISO audience.

# Ameen Product Overview

Status: Draft v1.0  
Owner: Product / Commercial  
Audience: Customers, partners, sales, onboarding

---

## Purpose

This document explains Ameen in clear customer language. It should be used as the foundation for customer presentations, onboarding material, website copy, and partner enablement.

Ameen is a Saudi AI Compliance Readiness Platform that helps organizations understand how ready they are against selected Saudi cybersecurity and governance frameworks by analyzing existing documents, mapping them to controls, identifying gaps, and guiding evidence collection.

Ameen does not replace the regulator, the auditor, the internal compliance team, or legal counsel. It supports readiness, evidence organization, and decision-making.

---

## Simple Explanation

Most organizations already have policies, procedures, reports, and evidence, but they do not know exactly which compliance controls these documents support.

Ameen allows the organization to upload those documents once. The platform analyzes the content, suggests control mappings, highlights what is covered, identifies what is missing, and creates a readiness view that humans can review and approve.

---

## Core Journey

The customer journey must remain simple:

1. Create organization.
2. Upload documents.
3. AI analyzes.
4. AI maps controls.
5. Customer reviews.
6. Customer uploads evidence.
7. Readiness dashboard updates.

This journey is the product. Internal complexity must never appear as customer complexity.

---

## What Ameen Helps Customers Answer

Ameen helps customers answer practical questions such as:

- Which controls appear covered by our current documents?
- Which controls are missing evidence?
- Which gaps should we prioritize first?
- What evidence should we collect?
- Which document supports each control?
- Why did the platform suggest this mapping?
- Are we improving over time?
- Are we ready enough for an internal review or external advisory assessment?

---

## Readiness Dimensions

Ameen separates readiness into four dimensions:

1. Documented: The requirement is addressed in a policy, procedure, standard, or document.
2. Implemented: The organization claims or demonstrates the control is operational.
3. Evidenced: Supporting artifacts exist and are attached.
4. Verified: A responsible human reviewer has checked and accepted the evidence.

This is important because a policy alone should not be presented as full compliance.

---

## What Ameen Is Not

Ameen is not:

- A regulator.
- A certification body.
- A legal opinion.
- A final audit opinion.
- A SIEM.
- A vulnerability scanner.
- An EDR tool.
- A ticketing system.
- A CMDB.

Ameen is the readiness intelligence layer that helps organizations understand and improve compliance evidence.

---

## AI Role

AI in Ameen assists with analysis and mapping. It does not approve compliance.

The human reviewer remains responsible for accepting or rejecting suggestions. Every customer-facing AI result should be explainable and should show why a control was suggested.

---

## Trust Rules

Ameen must always be honest about:

- Which frameworks are available today.
- Which frameworks are planned.
- Which content is official regulation.
- Which content is Ameen advisory guidance.
- Which AI model or analyzer is being used.
- Whether documents may leave the customer environment.
- What the platform can and cannot assure.

---

## First Commercial Position

The first commercial position is controlled pilot deployment.

Recommended pilot model:

- One customer.
- One deployment.
- Customer-controlled infrastructure where required.
- Local deterministic analyzer by default for sensitive environments.
- Limited document scope.
- Written statement of what Ameen assures and does not assure.
- Human review required before using outputs externally.

---

## Product Promise

Ameen makes compliance readiness understandable, traceable, and actionable.

The customer should feel that Ameen reduces confusion, improves evidence discipline, and gives leadership a clearer picture before an audit or readiness review.

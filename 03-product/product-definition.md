# Ameen Product Definition

## What Ameen Is

Ameen is an AI-powered compliance readiness platform for Saudi organizations.

It helps organizations assess readiness against Saudi regulatory frameworks by analyzing the documents and evidence they already have.

The product converts scattered documents into structured compliance insight:

- Which controls appear covered.
- Which controls are partially covered.
- Which controls have no evidence.
- Which evidence supports each conclusion.
- What gaps remain.
- What actions should be taken next.

## Product Category

Primary category:

> Saudi AI Compliance Readiness Platform

Secondary category:

> AI Cyber Governance Platform

Ameen is not merely a document repository, checklist tool, or dashboard. It is an intelligence layer that connects official frameworks, customer documents, evidence, human review, and readiness decisions.

## The Customer Journey

The customer journey is frozen as a strategic principle:

1. Create organization.
2. Upload documents.
3. AI analyzes the documents.
4. AI maps documents to controls.
5. Customer reviews the suggestions.
6. Customer uploads or links evidence.
7. Ameen shows readiness and gaps.

This journey must remain simple even as the internal system becomes more powerful.

## Core Product Capabilities

### 1. Official Framework Library

Ameen stores official Saudi framework content as controlled regulatory source material.

Official content must be traceable, versioned, and separated from Ameen advisory guidance.

### 2. Document Intelligence

Ameen extracts and analyzes uploaded customer documents including policies, procedures, standards, reports, and evidence files.

The product should support Arabic and English documents as first-class inputs.

### 3. Control Mapping

Ameen proposes which official controls are supported by which customer documents.

The mapping must include confidence, rationale, and traceable source passages.

### 4. Human Review

AI suggestions do not automatically create compliance.

A human reviewer must approve or reject material findings before readiness changes.

### 5. Evidence Management

Ameen helps customers attach evidence to controls, track evidence status, and support human verification.

### 6. Readiness Model

Ameen reports readiness using four dimensions:

- Documented
- Implemented
- Evidenced
- Verified

This prevents a policy document from being treated as full compliance.

### 7. Knowledge Layer

Ameen provides advisory guidance around official controls, including typical evidence, common gaps, assessment questions, and remediation suggestions.

This layer is Ameen's intellectual property and must remain clearly separate from official regulatory content.

### 8. Audit Traceability

Every important result should be traceable from control to document, evidence, reviewer, readiness calculation, and audit history.

## What Ameen Is Not

Ameen is not:

- A certification body.
- A regulator.
- A legal opinion.
- An audit firm.
- A SIEM.
- An EDR.
- A vulnerability scanner.
- A ticketing system.
- A full CMDB.
- A replacement for human review.

Ameen supports readiness. It does not guarantee regulatory acceptance.

## Primary Users

### CISO / Cybersecurity Leader

Wants to understand readiness, gaps, risk exposure, and audit preparation status.

### Compliance Manager

Wants to manage controls, evidence, findings, reviews, and improvement actions.

### Internal Auditor

Wants traceability, evidence packages, review history, and defensible results.

### IT / Infrastructure Owner

Wants clear evidence requests and technical remediation guidance.

### Executive Leadership

Wants a simple view of readiness, major gaps, risk impact, and progress.

### Consultant / Advisory Firm

Wants to accelerate assessment delivery, reduce manual review, and produce consistent output for clients.

## Primary Market

Ameen's first market is Saudi Arabia.

The first customer segments are:

- Government entities.
- Higher education.
- Healthcare.
- Large private organizations.
- Cybersecurity and GRC consulting firms.
- Managed service providers delivering compliance advisory.

## Product Promise

Ameen helps customers answer:

> Where do we stand, why, what evidence proves it, and what should we fix next?

## Product Success Criteria

Ameen is successful when it can consistently:

1. Use official frameworks correctly.
2. Read customer documents accurately.
3. Map evidence to controls with explainable reasoning.
4. Prevent AI from making unsupported compliance claims.
5. Help a human reviewer make faster and better decisions.
6. Produce readiness insights that a CISO and auditor can understand.
7. Preserve a simple experience for non-technical users.

## Strategic Product Rule

Every future feature must improve at least one of these:

- Customer trust.
- Assessment accuracy.
- Evidence quality.
- Audit traceability.
- Saudi framework coverage.
- Ease of use.
- Commercial readiness.

Features that do not improve one of these should not be prioritized.

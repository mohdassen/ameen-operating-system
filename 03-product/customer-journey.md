# Customer Journey

**Ameen Operating System**  
**Document Owner:** Product / Customer Success  
**Status:** Draft  
**Version:** 0.1

---

## Purpose

This document defines the official Ameen customer journey.

The journey must remain simple even if the underlying technology becomes complex.

---

## Core Journey

The official Ameen journey is:

> Create Organization → Upload Documents → AI Analyzes → AI Maps Controls → Customer Reviews → Upload Evidence → Readiness Dashboard

This journey should not be changed unless the product strategy is formally updated.

---

## Journey Principle

Ameen should feel simple to the customer.

The customer should not feel they are configuring a complicated GRC system. They should feel they are being guided by an intelligent compliance assistant that understands Saudi compliance and turns documents into readiness insight.

---

## Stage 1: Create Organization

### Customer Goal

Set up the organization or business unit that will be assessed.

### Customer Actions

- Enter organization name.
- Select organization type.
- Select applicable framework.
- Confirm basic assessment scope.

### Ameen Responsibilities

- Keep setup short.
- Avoid unnecessary questions.
- Explain why each required field matters.
- Avoid collecting fields that are not used.

### Success Indicator

Customer can start an assessment in minutes without needing consulting support.

---

## Stage 2: Upload Documents

### Customer Goal

Provide existing policies, procedures, and evidence for analysis.

### Customer Actions

- Upload policies.
- Upload procedures.
- Upload standards.
- Upload evidence documents if available.

### Ameen Responsibilities

- Accept common document types.
- Explain what documents are useful.
- Warn customers not to upload sensitive data if the deployment is not approved for it.
- Show upload status clearly.

### Success Indicator

Customer understands that Ameen starts from what they already have.

---

## Stage 3: AI Analyzes

### Customer Goal

Let Ameen classify and understand the uploaded documents.

### Customer Actions

- Wait for analysis.
- Review document classification.
- Correct classification if needed.

### Ameen Responsibilities

- Classify documents clearly.
- Explain confidence level where useful.
- Avoid pretending uncertain analysis is certain.
- Separate local deterministic analysis from external AI analysis.

### Success Indicator

Customer trusts that Ameen is reading documents intelligently, not randomly matching keywords.

---

## Stage 4: AI Maps Controls

### Customer Goal

Understand which controls the uploaded documents appear to support.

### Customer Actions

- Review proposed mappings.
- See why each mapping exists.
- Accept, reject, or adjust proposals.

### Ameen Responsibilities

- Show explainable mapping reasons.
- Avoid hidden decisions.
- Highlight gaps.
- Avoid claiming full compliance from document existence alone.

### Success Indicator

Customer sees a clear relationship between documents and controls.

---

## Stage 5: Customer Reviews

### Customer Goal

Convert AI suggestions into human-approved assessment decisions.

### Customer Actions

- Approve valid mappings.
- Reject weak mappings.
- Add comments.
- Assign follow-up actions if needed.

### Ameen Responsibilities

- Make approval simple.
- Preserve audit history.
- Distinguish AI-suggested from human-approved.
- Avoid making final compliance decisions automatically.

### Success Indicator

Customer remains in control of final readiness decisions.

---

## Stage 6: Upload Evidence

### Customer Goal

Attach proof that controls are not only documented but also implemented and evidenced.

### Customer Actions

- Upload evidence.
- Link evidence to controls.
- Add notes.
- Request verification if workflow supports it.

### Ameen Responsibilities

- Explain expected evidence.
- Support evidence reuse where appropriate.
- Avoid duplicate effort.
- Track evidence status.

### Success Indicator

Customer understands the difference between documentation and actual evidence.

---

## Stage 7: Readiness Dashboard

### Customer Goal

See current readiness, gaps, risks, and next actions.

### Customer Actions

- Review readiness score.
- Review missing controls.
- Review priority gaps.
- Export or share reports.

### Ameen Responsibilities

- Show readiness by dimension.
- Avoid misleading single-score compliance claims.
- Separate documented, implemented, evidenced, and verified states.
- Provide practical next actions.

### Success Indicator

Leadership can understand readiness without reading every document.

---

## Readiness Dimensions

Ameen should measure readiness across four dimensions:

1. Documented.
2. Implemented.
3. Evidenced.
4. Verified.

A policy alone should not create a complete compliance claim.

---

## Customer Emotions by Stage

### Before Ameen

- Confused.
- Pressured.
- Unsure.
- Dependent on spreadsheets.
- Worried about audit readiness.

### During Ameen

- Guided.
- Informed.
- In control.
- Able to see progress.

### After Ameen

- More confident.
- Better prepared.
- Clear on gaps.
- Able to brief leadership.

---

## Product Rule

Every new feature must answer this question:

> Does this make the core journey clearer, safer, faster, or more trustworthy?

If the answer is no, the feature should not be prioritized.

---

## Commercial Rule

Do not sell Ameen as magic.

Sell Ameen as the fastest way to turn existing documents and evidence into an explainable compliance readiness view.
